# Politics, Friday 13 November 2026

**The political news bearing on markets came through two channels: Washington's handling of the Nvidia affair, which has become a question of export controls and of the Commerce Secretary's role ahead of next week's Trump–Xi meeting, and a harder US line on Iran, which raised the risk premium in oil and gas. Diplomacy on Ukraine moved in the other direction, with reported progress after US envoys met President Putin.**

## United States

**Nvidia and export policy.** Lawmakers are pressing Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to explain why chip sales were allowed to a company linked to an alleged Chinese spy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/190e5eea-7461-4b26-8b69-17a3288e31d4)). China warned the US not to use the scandal as a pretext for new technology restrictions ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/66e7337e-3696-4259-84f4-57533bc185bd)). The market channel is the risk of tighter export controls on AI chips, which would weigh on semiconductor earnings beyond Nvidia, and a less predictable backdrop for the APEC meeting in Shenzhen on 18–19 November.

**Congress and fiscal policy.** After the Democrats' midterm gains, Senator Chris Van Hollen is challenging Chuck Schumer for the post of Senate majority leader ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/1f0996cf-1f84-42ca-a574-898f8578deb7)). Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said ending "tax gimmicks" is an option to reduce the deficit, depending on whether Democrats are serious about lowering debt ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/bessent-sees-ending-tax-gimmicks-as-option-to-trim-us-deficit)). With the 10-year yield at 5.10 per cent, the fiscal outlook bears directly on the term premium.

**Trade.** President Trump threatened to double tariffs on Mexico over illegal immigration ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/b4bb6e99-97c8-4392-a227-b7b74853ba26)); USD/CAD and the peso are the most exposed channels.

## Middle East and energy

President Trump wrote that the US does not need a deal with Iran ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)), which the FT reads as an end to Vice-President Vance's push for a peace agreement ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/3c1727bb-2966-4e5c-808b-873d14854fa2)). Bloomberg reports that the administration is betting that the present uneasy status quo will hold after the midterm loss ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/trump-bets-shaky-iran-status-quo-will-hold-after-midterm-loss)). Brent rose 3.3 per cent and TTF 6.5 per cent; for Norway the effect is higher petroleum revenue and support for the krone.

## Europe

**Ukraine.** President Zelenskyy said progress was made after US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met President Putin, citing a prisoner exchange ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/zelenskyy-hails-progress-after-witkoff-kushner-meet-putin)); Kushner said the US cannot reach a deal without Europe ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/8d28a5be-af56-421f-a33d-779a0fcc7ad4)). A credible ceasefire path would lower the risk premium in European gas.

**ECB succession and Italy.** Germany denied a report that President Macron and Chancellor Merz have agreed on Pablo Hernández de Cos to succeed Christine Lagarde ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/macron-merz-agree-de-cos-should-succeed-lagarde-le-point-says)). In Italy, Intesa's chief warned that banks may cut sovereign debt holdings in a dispute over taxation ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/6e27b96d-cb31-4e1a-bb1c-46816be25983)), a risk for the periphery spread, which stands at 0.59 percentage points.

## Asia

China's policy bank bonds sold off on concern about a possible tax on government bond interest ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/china-policy-bank-bonds-suffer-selloff-as-tax-worries-hit-market)). The US Treasury's use of about 500 million dollars' worth of euros to support the yen was confirmed by the New York Fed ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/us-confirms-500-million-spent-on-yen-intervention-with-euros)).

## Norway

No new political decisions with a market channel were found. The deadline set by the Labour Party for a 2027 budget agreement with its partners is 20 November.

## Dates to watch

- **17 November:** Nvidia's third-quarter results.
- **18–19 November:** APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen, with an expected Trump–Xi meeting.
- **20 November:** deadline for Norway's budget agreement.
