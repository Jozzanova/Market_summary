# Intraday, Europe open · Friday 13 November 2026, 09:05 Oslo time

**Europe opened slightly lower after a mixed Asian session, with the Nvidia leadership change still the main source of uncertainty, while Oslo Børs rose on higher oil and gas prices. The krone is a little stronger against the euro. Yahoo refused the refresh, so the prices below come from TradingView, delayed by up to 15 minutes, as of 09:06 Oslo time.**

## Equities
- **Asia was mixed.** The Nikkei is up 0.5 per cent, while the Hang Seng is down 1.6 per cent, the KOSPI 1.0 per cent and the Shanghai Composite 0.6 per cent. The Wall Street Journal describes the region's rebound as fading while [Nvidia uncertainty lingers](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/asia-stocks-stage-tentative-rebound-as-nvidia-uncertainty-lingers-c0953e15), and the Financial Times reports that a [Chinese AI sell-off deepened](https://www.ft.com/content/0d4e93e4-895e-4ae0-a01b-833a3ed440a8) on fears of tougher chip restrictions.
- **Europe is a little lower.** STOXX Europe 600 is down 0.3 per cent, the Euro Stoxx 50 0.4 per cent, the CAC 40 0.3 per cent and the FTSE 100 0.2 per cent.
- **Oslo Børs rose about 0.3 per cent early,** helped by oil and gas; Equinor is up 0.8 per cent and Okeanis 3.6 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/Qroo2P/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)).
- **US futures point higher:** S&P 500 futures are up 0.5 per cent, Nasdaq 100 futures down 0.1 per cent.

## Rates and currencies
- **Treasury yields are elevated.** The 10-year closed Thursday at 5.10 per cent, and the 5-year is 4.96 per cent this morning. Bloomberg writes that the surge has [shattered hopes that the worst of the bond rout is over](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/treasury-yield-surge-shatters-hopes-worst-of-bond-rout-is-over).
- **The dollar is firm.** EUR/USD is 1.106 and USD/JPY 159.90, close to the level at which Japan's finance minister has [warned of action](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/japan-s-katayama-warns-of-fx-steps-after-one-sided-rapid-moves).
- **The krone is slightly stronger against the euro.** EUR/NOK is 10.568 (down 0.3 per cent) and USD/NOK 9.555, little changed.

## Commodities
- **Oil is steady at a higher level.** Brent is 95.74 dollars and WTI 88.33, both slightly lower. Gold is up 0.5 per cent at 3,906 dollars; copper is down 0.7 per cent.
- **European gas is higher** at about 95 euros per megawatt-hour, according to E24 (link above).
