# Key points, Friday 13 November 2026

**Equities.** US equities ended slightly lower on Thursday after Nvidia confirmed that Jensen Huang had resigned as chief executive: the S&P 500 fell 0.35 per cent, the Nasdaq 0.39 per cent and the Russell 2000 1.66 per cent, having recovered most of an early fall of about 1 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Nvidia closed 4.3 per cent lower after touching a 52-week low during the session; SOX fell 0.54 per cent. STOXX Europe 600 rose 0.14 per cent and Oslo Børs was unchanged, with Equinor up 2.2 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/kMa45v/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). This morning the Nikkei is up 0.83 per cent, while the Hang Seng is down 0.93 per cent and the KOSPI 0.62 per cent after opening higher ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)).

**Government bonds.** Treasury yields rose markedly as Fed officials argued for further tightening: the 10-year rose 10.5 basis points to 5.10 per cent and the 5-year 12 basis points ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/treasury-yields-rise-as-fed-rate-hike-expectations-remain-strong-401fed89)). The official 2-year fix (4.73 per cent on 10 November) lags the move. German yields changed little. Fed funds futures price 27 basis points for 9 December and about 20 basis points more by January.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.2 and EUR/USD 1.106 (−0.3 per cent). USD/JPY crossed 160 on Thursday; Japan's finance minister warned of action ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/japan-s-katayama-warns-of-fx-steps-after-one-sided-rapid-moves)). EUR/NOK fell 0.21 per cent to 10.577 and I-44 0.39 per cent to 109.28, while USD/NOK rose 0.11 per cent to 9.563: higher oil supported the krone against the euro.

**Energy.** Brent settled 3.3 per cent higher at 95.82 dollars after President Trump wrote that the US does not need a deal with Iran ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)); it is 95.6 dollars this morning. TTF gas rose 6.5 per cent to 92.3 euros.

**Drivers.** Nvidia's leadership change, with chief financial officer Colette Kress as interim chief executive; Fed officials Williams, Daly and Hammack calling for further tightening ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/fed-s-williams-says-inflation-far-too-high-still-work-to-do)); jobless claims of 200,000, above expectations; and the end of the US push for an Iran deal ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/3c1727bb-2966-4e5c-808b-873d14854fa2)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.84 points at twelve months in government rates (3 basis points narrower) and 0.96 points at ten years. Statistics Norway's survey lowered the 2026 petroleum investment estimate by 9 billion kroner to 266 billion and put 2027 at 237 billion ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/Qrlp88/jekker-ned-aarets-oljeinvesteringer)). Professor Kjetil Storesletten argued that the policy rate should be 5–6 per cent ([Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/okonomi/rente-og-inflasjon-eksperter-frykter-tapt-tillit-til-norges-bank/s/80-95-240062)); no bank economist changed view, and the last reported pricing is about 35 per cent for a December hike.
