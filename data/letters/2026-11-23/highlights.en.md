# Key points, Monday 23 November 2026

**These reports cover Friday's session and the weekend. Friday's renewed fall in semiconductor shares completed the weakest week for US equities since the spring of 2025, and Treasuries again served as a haven; this morning US equity futures have recovered part of the loss while Seoul fell further, which suggests that the selling pressure has eased in the United States but not yet in the Asian chip makers.**

**Equities.** The S&P 500 fell 3.70 per cent on Friday, the Nasdaq 5.28 per cent and the Dow 1.93 per cent; the semiconductor index SOX fell 10.73 per cent and Nvidia 14 per cent after a report of an SEC review of its accounting ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html)). STOXX Europe 600 fell 2.00 per cent and Oslo Børs 1.50 per cent. This morning, with Tokyo closed, KOSPI is down 3.22 per cent, while Taiwan is up 2.1 per cent and S&P 500 futures 2.2 per cent ([Bloomberg](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/us-stock-futures-rally-after-ai-rout,-havens-fall:-markets-wrap/92421262)).

**Government bonds.** The US 5-year yield fell 16.5 basis points on Friday to 4.70 per cent and the 10-year 14 basis points to 4.91 per cent; Treasury futures fell about 0.2 per cent this morning as demand for havens eased. Fed funds futures price a Fed hike of 22.5 basis points on 9 December, about 90 per cent. The German 10-year fell 6 basis points to 3.39 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year (live) 6.5 basis points to 4.28 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.77, EUR/USD 1.0985 and USD/JPY 159.00. The krone weakened on Friday, with I-44 up 0.50 per cent to 110.74, as risk appetite fell; it is slightly firmer this morning, with EUR/NOK at 10.703 and USD/NOK at 9.744.

**Energy.** Brent's January contract is 92.29 dollars, about 0.3 per cent lower, as the equity fall weighed while attacks on Gulf shipping and a Saudi refinery limited the decline ([Reuters](https://www.asiaone.com/money/oil-prices-fall-amid-equity-selloff-though-iran-war-attacks-limit-losses)). TTF gas rose 4.7 per cent on Friday to 88.45 euros and rose further this morning on colder forecasts ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/european-gas-surges-as-cold-snap-threatens-tight-inventories)).

**Drivers.** Treasury Secretary Bessent said on Sunday that the administration still needs answers from Nvidia on export compliance ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bessent-nvidia-china-ai-chips-alice-huang-canard/)); China, Taiwan and South Korea introduced measures to support their equity markets. Gold rose 2.7 per cent to 3,610 dollars as the market pared bets on a December Fed hike.

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.78 percentage points at twelve months in government rates (Thursday's fix) and about 0.89 percentage point at ten years against Germany. No new comment on Norges Bank was found; the economists quoted expect a hold on 17 December. The red-green parties had not agreed on the 2027 budget by Sunday night and resume talks today ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/artikkel/statsbudsjettet-skisse-fra-ap-planlegger-nye-moter-mandag-1533707)).
