# Politics, Monday 23 November 2026

**This report covers Friday and the weekend. The weekend's political news affected markets mainly through the AI sector and energy: the US administration kept up the pressure on Nvidia over export compliance, Asian governments acted to support their equity markets, and attacks in the Gulf and on Russian shipping kept a risk premium in oil. In Norway, the red-green parties had not agreed on the 2027 budget by Sunday night.**

## United States

- **Nvidia.** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on CBS on Sunday that the administration still needs answers from Nvidia on whether it complied with export restrictions, and that the board should establish who was in charge of compliance; asked whether former chief executive Jensen Huang should return, he said "we have to see where the buck stops" ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bessent-nvidia-china-ai-chips-alice-huang-canard/)). The President has called for Huang's reinstatement ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/91b7a3a6-8ac5-4327-ad03-6d951f287346)). *Market channel:* regulatory uncertainty around the largest company in the index, which fell 14 per cent on Friday.
- **Congress.** House Democrats will start the new Congress with the largest majority this century and plan to use their investigative powers against the administration ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/9d2983a7-196d-4164-a0c1-e262f32fd8c9)). *Market channel:* AFP cites the prospect of tighter regulation under a Democrat-led Congress among the concerns weighing on technology shares ([AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261123-asian-tech-back-in-the-firing-line-as-traders-track-wall-st-rout)).

## Asia

- **Market support.** China eased margin requirements, Taiwan widened its short-selling curbs and said it has been buying shares, and South Korea eased limits on company buybacks for three months and is preparing to activate a stabilisation fund ([Bloomberg](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/us-stock-futures-rally-after-ai-rout,-havens-fall:-markets-wrap/92421262)). *Market channel:* the measures did not prevent a further fall in Seoul this morning, while Taiwan's market rose.

## Middle East, Russia and energy

- **Gulf.** The Houthi movement claimed an attack on a Saudi oil refinery on Sunday, and several attacks on shipping in the Gulf and near the Strait of Hormuz were reported over the weekend ([Reuters](https://www.asiaone.com/money/oil-prices-fall-amid-equity-selloff-though-iran-war-attacks-limit-losses)). *Market channel:* the risk to supply limited oil's decline this morning.
- **Russian shipping.** A sanctioned Russian cargo ship was hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday, and a Russian warship was sunk at a Libyan port; Ukraine is suspected ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/russian-ships-struck-in-attacks-far-from-ukraine-as-wars-fallout-spreads-a51964cb)). *Market channel:* higher insurance and freight risk for Russian seaborne trade.
- **Climate talks.** The COP31 talks in Turkey ended on Sunday without agreement, the first such failure since 2009, after producers opposed a statement on phasing out fossil fuels ([Reuters](https://www.asiaone.com/money/oil-prices-fall-amid-equity-selloff-though-iran-war-attacks-limit-losses)).

## Europe

- **Spain.** The People's Party has pledged broad tax cuts if it wins the general election on 29 November ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/spain-election-frontrunner-pledges-tax-cuts-and-financial-audit)). *Market channel:* the fiscal outlook and the Spanish spread.
- **Trade.** The EU signed an agreement with Indonesia that removes most tariffs between the two ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/trade/eu-inks-trade-deal-with-indonesia-to-cut-tariffs-catalyze-investment-9810d41f)).

## Norway

- **Budget.** Labour presented a written outline to Rødt, SV, MDG and Sp on Sunday evening, but no agreement was reached, and talks resume at 09:00 today ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/artikkel/statsbudsjettet-skisse-fra-ap-planlegger-nye-moter-mandag-1533707)). Sp demands lower fuel taxes, and MDG demands real emission cuts in 2027 ([TV 2](https://www.tv2.no/nyheter/kan-ga-mot-budsjettenighet-i-dag/19457346/)). *Market channel:* what matters for Norges Bank and the krone is the spending of oil revenue; no report has given a figure for how an agreement would change it.
