# Politics, weekly report week 47 (13–20 November 2026)

**Politics affected markets this week mainly through technology: export controls, disclosures about chip sales to China, a regulatory probe of Nvidia and the President's threat to tax data centres built abroad all added to the pressure on AI shares. The leaders' meeting in Shenzhen produced few concrete results, and the conflict with Iran kept a risk premium in oil without preventing a weekly fall in prices. In Norway, the red-green parties missed their own deadline for a budget agreement.**

## United States

- **Chips and China.** New licence requirements for advanced chips sent to data centres serving Chinese companies were announced on Monday ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6222897-bis-limits-remote-computing-exports/amp/)). On Thursday the House Foreign Affairs Committee published data showing that about 879,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips were approved for Chinese companies between December 2025 and May 2026 ([Reuters](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/19/us-signed-off-on-sales-of-nearly-900000-ai-chips-to-china-in-six-months)), and California's attorney general has subpoenaed Nvidia over whether its chips reached China ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/20/global-stock-markets-slump-anthropic-ipo-ai-sell-off)). On Friday Reuters reported that SEC enforcement staff are examining Nvidia's accounting; the SEC declined to comment ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html)). *Market channel:* regulatory risk for the largest AI companies, whose earnings carry much of the index; Nvidia fell about 23 per cent over the week.
- **Data centres.** At a press conference on Friday President Trump said US companies would face a "tremendous tax" if they build data centres abroad; he gave no legal basis, and the announcement appears to have surprised his own staff ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/trump-data-centers-ai-companies-tax.html)). *Market channel:* higher costs and less flexibility for the cloud providers whose investment drives demand for chips.
- **Fiscal deadlines.** The stopgap funds the federal government until 11 December ([Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R49353)); the Senate passed a bipartisan bill to speed approval of energy infrastructure ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/senate-bipartisan-permitting-reform-bill-energy-baaja-trump/)).

## Asia

- **Shenzhen summit.** Presidents Trump, Xi and Putin agreed on Thursday to bring more fuel to market and to reduce nuclear risks; the statement does not mention Ukraine or Iran, and no volumes were given ([AP](https://globalnews.ca/news/12157687/trump-xi-and-putin-reach-agreements-on-oil-and-nuclear-risks)). *Market channel:* a supply pledge without figures weighs on oil only at the margin.

## Middle East and energy

- **Iran and the Gulf.** Iran attacked a US base in Bahrain on the weekend of 15 November, and the United States struck Qeshm Island on Tuesday ([AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261118-stocks-drop-as-booming-nvidia-results-drowned-by-spy-scandal)); a Houthi attack damaged a tower in Riyadh's financial district ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-houthi-missiles-targeting-riyadh-and-south)). According to the Financial Times, the administration is considering new strikes on Iran ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html)). *Market channel:* the attacks kept a risk premium in oil, but the IEA's estimate of a supply surplus dominated, and Brent fell 3.4 per cent over the week.

## Europe

- **EU–UK.** Prime Minister Andy Burnham met EU leaders in Brussels on Friday to conclude agreements on food-trade checks, emissions trading and a youth scheme ([AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261120-eu-and-uk-eye-reset-deal-at-burnham-s-brussels-summit)).
- **Spain.** Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez faces opposition leader Alberto Núñez Feijóo in the general election on 29 November ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/spain-s-sanchez-struggles-in-debate-against-main-rival-feijoo)). *Market channel:* the periphery spread, which was little changed over the week.

## Norway

- **Budget.** The red-green parties did not reach agreement on the 2027 budget by the deadline at 23:00 on Friday; the talks continue over the weekend, with the aim of an agreement before the Storting meets on Monday. Sp demands lower fuel taxes, which MDG and SV oppose, and the government proposed spending just over 600 billion kroner of oil revenue, 2.7 per cent of the fund ([NTB](https://www.tv2.no/nyheter/forhandlinger-om-statsbudsjettet-fortsetter-pa-overtid/19454639/)). *Market channel:* the spending of oil revenue is what matters for Norges Bank and the krone; no report has given a figure for how an agreement would change it.
- **Oil fields.** The Supreme Court ruling that the approvals of three oil fields must be reassessed has entered the budget talks ([NTB](https://www.adressa.no/nyheter/innenriks/i/z0GgVw/mdg-krever-full-oljestans-etter-klimadom)).
