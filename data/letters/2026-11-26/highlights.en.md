# Key points, Thursday 26 November 2026

**A sharp intraday fall in US technology shares was largely reversed before the close, and Asian markets have recovered this morning as investors look past the proposed US tariffs. Core PCE inflation above expectations and upward-revised GDP growth confirmed the pricing of a Fed hike on 9 December, but yields fell as investors sought safety during the sell-off. US markets are closed today for Thanksgiving.**

**Equities.** The S&P 500 fell 0.25 per cent after a decline of as much as 3.2 per cent during the day, and the Nasdaq 0.62 per cent, while the Dow rose 0.33 per cent ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-november-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-pce-inflation-data-091428237.html)); the SOX fell 0.65 per cent and Nvidia about 4 per cent ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Business/stocks-tumble-tech-sell-off-resumes-nvidia-falls/story?id=138442070)). STOXX Europe 600 fell 1.06 per cent, with banks down about 3 per cent as the ECB discussed higher unremunerated reserve requirements ([Reuters](https://longbridge.com/en/news/306829837)). Oslo Børs was unchanged, where Norse Atlantic fell 85 per cent after warning that its financial situation is critical ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/dJOVqJ/nedgang-paa-oslo-boers)). This morning the Nikkei is up 1.53 per cent, KOSPI 1.70 per cent and the Hang Seng 1.86 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/stock-markets-today-.html)). The VIX rose 1.9 points to 28.3.

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield fell 5.6 basis points to 4.85 per cent and the 5-year 5.2 basis points to 4.66 per cent, as demand for safe assets outweighed the firm data. Fed funds futures imply a Fed hike of 23.6 basis points on 9 December. The German 10-year fell 3 basis points to 3.30 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year (live) 4 basis points to 4.12 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.82, EUR/USD 1.0963 and USD/JPY 159.18. The krone was little changed this morning, with EUR/NOK at 10.741 and USD/NOK at 9.800, while I-44 rose 0.39 per cent at Wednesday's fix to 111.47, probably as risk appetite fell during the sell-off.

**Energy.** The January Brent contract rose 3.5 per cent to 94.61 dollars on Wednesday after the Houthis claimed attacks on the Ras Tanura refinery ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/oil-wti-brent-hormuz-middle-east.html)); the front-month series shows 91.15 dollars this morning after the change of contract month. TTF gas fell 4.76 per cent to 87.30 euros on milder weather forecasts ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/european-natural-gas-prices-drop-amid-forecasts-for-milder-temperatures-5050476)).

**Drivers.** Core PCE rose 3.3 per cent over twelve months against 3.2 per cent expected, and Q3 GDP growth was revised up to 4.3 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/25/pce-inflation-report-october-2026.html)), while the Beige Book reported flat consumer spending ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202611-summary.htm)). USTR proposed tariffs of 15 to 35 per cent on fourteen economies, but none before late January ([USTR](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural)). The Bank of Korea raised its policy rate to 3.25 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/bok-rate-decision-korea-gdp-inflation.html)), and BoJ board member Naoki Tamura argued for swift hikes ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/bojs-tamura-signals-chance-of-fasterpace-interest-rate-hikes-5051855)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.83 percentage points at twelve months in government rates and about 0.82 percentage point at ten years. LFS unemployment rose to 4.7 per cent in October ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/arbeid-og-lonn/sysselsetting/statistikk/arbeidskraftundersokelsen)). No new comment on Norges Bank was found; the economists quoted expect a hold on 17 December. Four parties agreed on the 2027 budget, raising the use of oil revenue by 7.4 billion kroner, but without a majority ([Aftenposten](https://www.aftenposten.no/norge/politikk/i/0vn14B/fire-partier-enige-om-statsbudsjett-kutter-i-drivstoffavgift)). Third-quarter national accounts are due at 08:00; Norges Bank projected mainland growth of 0.3 per cent.
