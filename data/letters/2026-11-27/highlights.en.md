# Key points, Friday 27 November 2026

**With New York closed for Thanksgiving, European equities and metals recovered as the US capped its proposed tariffs at the rates in existing trade deals and technology shares rebounded; the recovery has continued in Asia this morning. The ECB account showed broad support for a hike already in October, while mainland growth in Norway was weaker than Norges Bank expected.**

**Equities.** STOXX Europe 600 rose 1.75 per cent and the DAX 2.08 per cent, with all major sectors higher and Imperial Brands up 13.3 per cent on bid interest ([Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/european-stocks-rally-tech-banks-083233000.html)). Oslo Børs rose 1.46 per cent, led by Kongsberg Gruppen and Norsk Hydro, while Thor Medical fell 19.4 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/yz3adx/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). US markets were closed and trade a shortened session today. This morning the Nikkei is up 1.37 per cent, KOSPI 2.49 per cent and the Hang Seng 1.10 per cent.

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield is 4.91 per cent, 6.4 basis points above Wednesday's close, as demand for safe assets eased ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-27/treasuries-retreat-as-gains-in-equities-sap-demand-for-havens)); the 5-year has no new print. Fed funds futures still imply a Fed hike of 23.9 basis points on 9 December. The German 10-year rose 2 basis points to 3.32 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year (live) 6 basis points to 4.18 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.75, EUR/USD 1.0973 and USD/JPY 159.30. The krone strengthened: EUR/NOK fell 0.64 per cent to 10.680, USD/NOK 0.46 per cent to 9.734 and I-44 0.56 per cent to 110.84 at Thursday's fix, as risk appetite returned and oil was supported.

**Energy.** Brent for January delivery is about 96 dollars, 3.9 per cent higher over the week ([Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/shares-footing-bruising-month-dollar-015346000.html)), supported by a deadly drone attack in Kuwait and Iranian claims of mine strikes on tankers near Hormuz ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-military-ai-weapons-oil/)). TTF gas rose 0.90 per cent to 88.09 euros.

**Drivers.** USTR plans to cap the new tariffs at deal rates, 15 per cent for the EU. Euro-area leaders recommended Pablo Hernández de Cos as ECB President ([Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/26/european-council-puts-forward-pablo-hernandez-de-cos-as-next-president-of-the-european-central-bank/)), and the ECB account shows that a large number of members could have supported a hike in October ([ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/accounts/2026/html/ecb.mg261126~ccf28c8071.en.html)). Tokyo core inflation reached a sixteen-month high, supporting a BoJ hike in December. Gold rose 3.49 per cent and copper 5.64 per cent with the rebound in technology shares.

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.83 percentage points at twelve months in government rates and about 0.86 percentage point at ten years against Germany. Mainland GDP grew 0.1 per cent in the third quarter against Norges Bank's 0.3 per cent, with household consumption up 1.6 per cent ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/nasjonalregnskap-og-konjunkturer/nasjonalregnskap/statistikk/nasjonalregnskap/artikler/svak-vekst-i-fastlandsokonomien)); Kyrre M. Knudsen of SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge sees less pressure in the economy ([NTB](https://sol.no/nyheter/positivt-for-alle-som-onsker-rentekutt/85473160)). None of the economists quoted expects a hike on 17 December. Retail trade and NAV unemployment are due at 08:00.
