# Politics, Sunday 15 November 2026

**The weekend's political news bears on markets mainly through the Gulf and the week of summit diplomacy in Asia. A new Iranian attack on Bahrain on Sunday morning and US estimates that Iran has rebuilt part of its missile stock raise the risk to oil supply, while the ASEAN, APEC and Trump–Xi–Putin meetings this week will set the tone for trade and chip policy. In Norway, an analysis for the energy ministry quantifies how a rising CO₂ tax could shorten field lives.**

## Middle East and energy

**Bahrain.** Bahrain warned the public to take cover during an Iranian attack on Sunday morning; the WSJ describes it as an escalation of the conflict ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-attacks-bahrain-as-conflict-escalates-62ffd1b3)). US officials estimate that Iran still has up to 2,500 missiles, contrary to the President's statements, and the reconstitution of the stockpile has entered the administration's discussion of whether to resume strikes ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/u-s-figures-iran-still-has-up-to-2-500-missiles-countering-trump-assertions-4c6f4d67)). The market channel is the risk premium in oil: Brent fell 2.5 per cent last week as the IEA found a surplus, and a renewed threat to Gulf shipments would work in the other direction, supporting the krone through the terms of trade.

## United States

**Air traffic.** Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said Friday's four-hour nationwide ground stop arose when a contractor switched telecommunications lines at a remote facility; more than 8,000 flights were cancelled on Friday and over 4,000 by Saturday morning, and the FAA expects full schedules by the close of Sunday ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/14/faa-ground-stop-flights-resume-airport-chaos)). Airline shares will reflect the disruption on Monday.

**Trade and defence.** Bloomberg reports that the rise of Stephen Miller in the White House has unsettled Mexican trade negotiators ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/miller-s-white-house-ascent-rattles-mexico-trade-negotiators)), and that the Pentagon's cuts to US troops in Europe will be smaller than European leaders had feared ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/pentagon-eyes-smaller-troop-cuts-across-europe-in-reversal)), which eases somewhat the pressure on European defence budgets.

## Europe

**Ukraine.** Jared Kushner, who has served as a US negotiator with Russia and Ukraine, said the administration wants direct talks between Russia and Europe ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-14/kushner-wants-direct-russia-europe-talks-in-ukraine-peace-push)). Progress towards a settlement would bear on European gas prices, where TTF remains about three times its level at the start of the year.

## Asia

**Summit week.** Trump attends the ASEAN meeting in the Philippines from Sunday before the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen from Wednesday and the trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin; AI supply chains, tariffs and the South China Sea are on the agenda ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/15/asia-diplomatic-summits-trump-xi-putin-apec-asean)). Australia and the Philippines signed a new defence pact ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/australia-philippines-upgrade-defense-ties-with-focus-on-china)). In Malaysia, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's coalition lost a third consecutive regional election ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/anwar-s-coalition-suffers-third-straight-regional-poll-defeat)).

## Norway

**CO₂ tax and the shelf.** Wood Mackenzie, in an analysis for the energy ministry, finds that a high CO₂ tax on top of EU emission allowances could close fields earlier and leave up to two billion barrels of oil equivalent in the ground; production could be 11–13 per cent lower in 2040 with a high tax than with a low one ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/kMJR06/analyse-avgift-kan-stenge-oljefelt-tidlig)). The government will take the analysis into the budget negotiations and the petroleum white paper due in 2027. Together with Friday's Supreme Court ruling, it points to a gradually lower petroleum contribution to activity over the long term.

**Budget.** Labour's deadline for a 2027 budget agreement is Friday 20 November; no report of a settlement could be found.
