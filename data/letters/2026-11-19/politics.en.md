# Politics, Thursday 19 November 2026

**The trilateral meeting of Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen today is the political event of the week, but expectations are modest and no outcome was known this morning. The conflict with Iran continued to affect shipping in the Gulf, yet the oil market gave more weight to rising inventories. In Norway, the budget talks enter their last two days before Labour's deadline.**

## United States, China and Russia

- **Shenzhen.** Trump and Putin held their first in-person meeting in more than a year on the sidelines of the APEC gathering this morning ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/trump-meets-with-russia-s-putin-for-first-time-in-over-a-year)), ahead of the three-way meeting with Xi Jinping, whose agenda includes the Iran war and possible nuclear arms talks ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-xi-putin-china-apec.html)). Trump said he would not make agreements with Putin without Ukraine ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/trump-says-he-wouldn-t-make-deals-with-putin-without-ukraine)). The market channels are sanctions on Russian energy, the oil risk premium and US–China trade, where the truce expires in January.
- **Chips.** Trump suggested the United States could allow sales of Nvidia's Blackwell chips to China in the future ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/trump-suggests-us-can-sell-blackwell-chips-to-china-in-future)). The statement concerns future export controls rather than current policy, but it bears on the revenue outlook of US chip makers, which have fallen markedly this month.
- **Trade.** Thailand said the United States has agreed to lower an additional tariff on Thai goods to 10 per cent from 12.5 per cent ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/thailand-says-us-agrees-to-lower-forced-labor-tariff-to-10)).

## Middle East and energy

The United States struck a Revolutionary Guards missile site on Qeshm Island, Iran targeted US sites in Kuwait, and a projectile hit a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran says the strait is closed ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-iran-war-hormuz-oil-.html)). Outbound LNG tanker traffic through the strait appears to have dropped over the last week ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/lng-exports-through-hormuz-appear-to-slow-after-more-attacks)). The channel is the risk premium in oil and gas, but on Wednesday it was outweighed by higher US inventories and expectations of a global surplus: Brent fell 1.3 per cent and TTF gas 5.3 per cent ([Reuters](https://www.myjoyonline.com/oil-prices-settle-lower-as-traders-weigh-surplus-supply-outlook/)). Lower LNG flows from the Gulf would, if sustained, raise the value of Norwegian gas exports to Europe.

## United States, domestic

The health insurers fell 10 to 13 per cent after the administration's Medicare agency proposed wider audits of Medicare Advantage plans ([Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/trading-day-markets-mixed-nvidia-221528504.html)), an example of how regulatory decisions are moving single sectors. Republican Nick Begich held Alaska's House seat ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/gop-s-begich-holds-alaska-house-seat-resisting-democratic-tide)), one of the last midterm results. Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth signed a new military agreement with Venezuela's interim government ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/americas/hegseth-cements-new-military-deal-in-visit-to-venezuela-621ccdcb)).

## Europe

At a NATO meeting on support for Ukraine, a European defence chief rebuked a Pentagon official over criticism of Kyiv's strikes ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/european-defense-chief-snaps-at-pentagon-official-over-ukraine-criticism-0f47f082)). Transatlantic friction on Ukraine bears on European defence spending and thereby on fiscal policy and bond supply.

## Norway

The talks between Labour and the Centre Party, the Socialist Left, Red and the Greens continue towards Labour's deadline at 23:00 on Friday. The Centre Party is demanding a cut in fuel taxes, Red wants the fixed power price (Norgespris) at 40 øre rather than the government's 45 øre, and wealth tax remains disputed; no agreement had been reported ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nyheter/statsbudsjettet-2027-1.18034953)). The channel for markets is the fiscal stance and, through fuel taxes and Norgespris, measured CPI. Equinor said on Wednesday that work to contain the cyber-attack on certain IT systems continues, with no effect on production ([Equinor](https://www.equinor.com/no/nyheter/20261711-equinor-har-avdekket-cyberangrep)).
