# Key points, Thursday 19 November 2026

**Equities.** The S&P 500 fell 0.23 per cent and the Dow 1.11 per cent, while the Nasdaq rose 0.42 per cent; Nvidia fell 2.9 per cent despite results above expectations, and Humana and UnitedHealth fell 10 to 13 per cent after a proposal for wider audits of Medicare Advantage ([Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/trading-day-markets-mixed-in-a-nvidia-anthropic-minutes-swirl-ce785edddf8bf621)). STOXX Europe 600 fell 1.02 per cent, led by miners as copper fell ([Reuters](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/european-stocks-hit-two-week-low-on-weak-miners-caution-ahead-of-anthropic-ipo/article_7b8eb40e-039b-5bde-8cf3-40d8fa4999fd.html)), and Oslo Børs 1.05 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/bors/nyheter/a/306283?pinnedEntry=306283)). This morning the Hang Seng is down 1.89 per cent, while the Nikkei is up 0.34 per cent.

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield fell 3.7 basis points to 5.07 per cent and the 5-year 1.6 basis points to 4.88 per cent, while the 2-year rose slightly, so the curve flattened ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/treasury-yields-bonds-fed.html)). CME FedWatch shows a 95 per cent probability of a 25 basis point Fed hike on 9 December. The German 10-year rose 1 basis point to 3.43 per cent, and the Norwegian 10-year (live) fell 4.7 basis points to 4.36 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.92, EUR/USD 1.0967 and USD/JPY 159.57. The krone weakened: EUR/NOK is 10.658, 0.76 per cent higher, USD/NOK 9.720 and I-44 109.92 at Wednesday's fix, as lower oil prices and weaker risk appetite weighed.

**Energy.** Brent settled 1.3 per cent lower at 91.95 dollars after a larger-than-expected rise in US crude stocks ([Reuters](https://www.myjoyonline.com/oil-prices-settle-lower-as-traders-weigh-surplus-supply-outlook/)) and is quoted at 89.24 dollars this morning. TTF gas fell 5.3 per cent to 80.85 euros.

**Drivers.** Almost all FOMC participants judged further increases likely to be appropriate ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20261028.htm)); Christopher Waller said a 50 basis point hike is "probably necessary" in December ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/federal-reserve-governor-christopher-waller.html)); UK inflation rose to 3.6 per cent, below the Bank of England's forecast ([ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices/bulletins/consumerpriceinflation/october2026)); Australian unemployment rose to 4.9 per cent ([ABS](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/labour/employment-and-unemployment/labour-force-australia/latest-release)); Anthropic priced its listing at 83 dollars a share ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html)); Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen today.

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.80 percentage points at twelve months in government rates and 0.97 percentage points at ten years. Labour costs in manufacturing rose 4.7 per cent from a year earlier in the third quarter ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/arbeid-og-lonn/lonn-og-arbeidskraftkostnader/statistikk/arbeidskraftkostnadsindeks)). No new comments on Norges Bank were found; the economists quoted still expect a hold on 17 December. The Expectations Survey is published at 10:00.
