# Intraday update, Friday 6 November 2026, as of 09:35 Oslo time

**Summary.** European equities opened lower after Thursday's fall on Wall Street and the 6 per cent declines in Tokyo and Seoul overnight, but the losses have not widened in the first hour and a half: the STOXX Europe 600 is down 1.4 per cent. Oslo Børs is trading with a technical fault in its benchmark index. Treasuries, oil and the dollar are little changed to slightly firmer, and the krone is about 0.3 per cent weaker against the euro. The US employment report at 14:30 is the next test.
