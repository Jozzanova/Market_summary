# Markets letter, Friday 6 November 2026

**Summary.** The sell-off in technology shares that began on Wall Street on Thursday spread to Asia this morning. The S&P 500 fell 4.4 per cent and the Nasdaq 5.5 per cent on Thursday, the largest daily declines since April 2025, on a reassessment of the regulatory outlook for AI data centres after the Democratic sweep of Congress and weak guidance from chip makers. In Tokyo the Nikkei 225 fell close to 6 per cent and entered a bear market; in Seoul the KOSPI briefly fell more than 8 per cent and trading was halted by a circuit breaker. Treasury yields are lower, oil is near 100 dollars on further tanker incidents in the Strait of Hormuz, and the krone is little changed after three days of weakening.

## Equities

- **United States.** The S&P 500 closed at 7,354 (−4.4 per cent), the Nasdaq Composite at 24,315 (−5.5 per cent) and the Dow at 51,523 (−3.4 per cent). The technology sector fell 8.0 per cent and no sector rose. Nvidia lost about 9 per cent and turned negative for the year; Micron and AMD fell about 17 per cent. The VIX closed at 28.2, up from 16.6. After hours, Airbnb rose 11 per cent on results and DraftKings fell 24 per cent; index futures were mixed overnight, with Nasdaq-100 futures higher and Dow futures lower. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-5-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-plunge-chip-stocks-melt-down-193726491.html))
- **Europe.** The STOXX Europe 600 fell 0.4 per cent to 654.9, the DAX and FTSE 100 each 0.4 per cent, while the CAC 40 rose 0.3 per cent. Technology names followed the US lower and Rheinmetall fell about 17 per cent after cutting its sales forecast. The US session's losses came after the European close, so Europe opens today with ground to make up. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tech-drag-defence-weakness-weigh-164047028.html))
- **Norway.** The benchmark index fell 1.0 per cent to 2,081, still 23 per cent higher for the year. BP sold about 5 per cent of Aker BP at a 12 per cent discount; Aker BP fell 9.8 per cent. ([E24](https://e24.no/bors/nyheter/a/306142?pinnedEntry=306142))
- **Asia this morning.** The Nikkei 225 was down 5.9 per cent at 57,481 in the latest print, more than 20 per cent below its June peak; Advantest and SoftBank fell about 11 to 12 per cent. The KOSPI fell as much as 8.6 per cent, below 5,000 for the first time in nine months, and the Korea Exchange suspended trading for 20 minutes; it has since recovered to −5.2 per cent. TSMC fell close to 10 per cent in Taipei. The Hang Seng (−1.1 per cent) and Shanghai Composite (−1.1 per cent) were less affected. Closing levels were not available at the time of writing. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/business/2026/11/06/japans-stock-benchmark-nikkei-tumbles-more-than-6-after-trading-opens/), [Gujarat Samachar](https://english.gujaratsamachar.com/news/business/kospi-and-nikkei-crash-as-wall-streets-ai-carnage-triggers-massive-asian-stock-meltdown-59652549048))

## Fixed income and credit

- The US 10-year Treasury yield fell 6 basis points to 4.84 per cent and the 5-year 8 basis points to 4.61 per cent. Fed funds futures price about a 70 per cent probability of a 25 basis point hike on 9 December, down from more than 80 per cent on Wednesday. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- The euro-area AAA 10-year yield was 3.34 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year 4.37 per cent at Wednesday's fixes. US high-yield spreads were 332 basis points on 4 November; the official fixes do not yet reflect Thursday's equity fall.

## Commodities and currencies

- Brent trades at 99.7 dollars (+1.5 per cent) and WTI at 91.6 dollars after Iran's Revolutionary Guard reported further tanker fires in the Strait of Hormuz. Gold has eased 1.4 per cent to 4,056 dollars and copper is little changed at 6.23 dollars per pound after Thursday's 3 per cent fall. ([Al Arabiya](https://english.alarabiya.net/amp/News/middle-east/2026/11/05/iran-says-two-tankers-and-tugboat-caught-fire-in-strait-of-hormuz))
- The dollar index is 0.2 per cent firmer at 101.8. The Australian dollar has fallen 1.4 per cent with Asian equities, and Bitcoin 6.1 per cent to about 75,500 dollars. EUR/NOK is at 10.43, near Thursday's Norges Bank fix of 10.44, against 10.32 on Monday. On Norges Bank's import-weighted index I-44 the krone was fixed at 107.76 on Thursday, 0.5 per cent weaker on the day but 10.1 per cent stronger than at the start of the year, and close to Tuesday's 106.65, the strongest level in a year ([Norges Bank](https://data.norges-bank.no/api/data/EXR/B.I44.NOK.SP?format=csv&lastNObservations=10)).
