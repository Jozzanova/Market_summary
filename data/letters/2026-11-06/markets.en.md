# Markets letter, Friday 6 November 2026

**Summary.** The sell-off in technology shares that began on Wall Street on Thursday spread to Asia overnight and to Europe this morning. The S&P 500 fell 4.4 per cent and the Nasdaq Composite 5.5 per cent on Thursday, the largest daily declines since April 2025, as investors reassessed the regulatory outlook for AI data centres after the Democratic sweep of Congress. The Nikkei 225 closed 6.0 per cent lower and the KOSPI 6.0 per cent lower; European indices opened about 1.5 per cent down. Oslo Børs is affected by a technical fault in its benchmark index this morning. Treasury yields are little changed after Thursday's fall, Brent is near 98 dollars and the krone has weakened further, with EUR/NOK at 10.48. The US employment report at 14:30 is the main event of the day.

## Equities

- **United States.** The S&P 500 closed at 7,354 (−4.4 per cent), the Nasdaq at 24,315 (−5.5 per cent) and the Dow at 51,523 (−3.4 per cent). The technology sector fell 8.0 per cent and no sector rose. The AI complex fell much further than the market: the Philadelphia semiconductor index (SOX) lost 12.3 per cent and is 22 per cent lower over a week, NYSE FANG+ fell 6.0 per cent, the Magnificent Seven ETF 4.9 per cent and the AI ETFs between 5.9 and 6.6 per cent. Nvidia lost about 9 per cent; Micron and AMD about 17 per cent. The VIX closed at 28.2, against 16.6 on Wednesday. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-5-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-plunge-chip-stocks-melt-down-193726491.html), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/642b32dc-e3a1-4df8-ae91-5c84bdeb89e0))
- **Asia.** The Nikkei 225 closed at 57,435, down 6.0 per cent, its largest fall since April 2025 and 18 per cent below its level a month ago. The KOSPI closed at 5,060 (−6.0 per cent) after an intraday fall of more than 8 per cent triggered a 20-minute trading halt. The Hang Seng (−1.3 per cent) and the Shanghai Composite (−1.1 per cent) were less affected. ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/nikkei-falls-5-9-dragged-by-chip-stocks-d8937761), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/b77ebe43-a017-4e63-af84-5bdec8f47648), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/ai-selloff-asian-stock-markets-nikkei-kospi-taiex-grim-milestones))
- **Europe.** In early trading the STOXX Europe 600 is down 1.5 per cent at 645, the DAX 1.7 per cent, the FTSE 100 1.5 per cent and the CAC 40 1.4 per cent. Europe absorbed only part of the US fall on Thursday, since most of it came after the European close.
- **Norway.** The benchmark index closed Thursday at 2,081 (−1.0 per cent), 23 per cent higher for the year. This morning Oslo Børs has confirmed a technical fault affecting the benchmark index; the OBX index of the 25 most traded shares is down about 1.5 per cent, with Equinor down 1.9 per cent and Frontline 2.8 per cent, while Orkla rose after its third-quarter results. ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/rMv53w/boersene-skjelver-oslo-i-ekstrem-sweet-spot))

## Fixed income and credit

- The US 10-year yield fell 6 basis points to 4.84 per cent on Thursday and the 5-year 8 basis points to 4.61 per cent. Treasuries are little changed this morning, with the initial post-election rally fading ahead of the payroll data. ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/u-s-treasurys-little-changed-markets-await-employment-data-88f3f2b6))
- The Norwegian 10-year yield fell 6 basis points to 4.31 per cent at Thursday's fix and the 3-year to 4.60 per cent. The euro-area AAA 10-year was 3.34 per cent on Wednesday. US high-yield spreads were 332 basis points on 4 November; the official fixes do not yet reflect Thursday's equity fall.

## Commodities and currencies

- Brent traded above 100 dollars in Asian hours on renewed threats to shipping in the Gulf and is at 98.3 dollars, unchanged on the day; WTI is at 90.4 dollars. Gold has eased 1.2 per cent to 4,064 dollars and copper is little changed at 6.23 dollars per pound. ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/brent-crude-climbs-above-100-as-middle-east-oil-supply-risks-persist-caaf64bb))
- The dollar index is 0.3 per cent firmer at 101.9 and EUR/USD 0.4 per cent lower at 1.119. The Australian dollar has fallen 1.8 per cent with Asian equities.
- The krone has weakened with risk appetite: EUR/NOK is at 10.48 (+0.4 per cent) and USD/NOK at 9.37 (+0.8 per cent). Norges Bank's import-weighted index I-44 was 107.76 at Thursday's fix, 0.5 per cent weaker on the day but 10 per cent stronger than at the start of the year. EUR/SEK has risen 0.8 per cent.
