# Norges Bank letter, Friday 6 November 2026

**Summary.** Norges Bank kept the policy rate at 4.50 per cent on Thursday, as all 26 economists in Reuters' poll expected ([RTÉ/Reuters](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1105/1598078-norway-rates-kept-on-hold/)), and repeated that it is prepared to raise the rate if needed. The Norwegian press and bank economists read the decision as a hold with a door left open: most now think the peak has been reached, mainly because the krone has appreciated by about 12 per cent this year, but several warn that a weaker krone or a high October CPI on Tuesday could bring a hike back into view in December. The Governor, interviewed after the decision, pointed to the krone's effect on inflation and to rising, debt-financed AI investment as a source of higher rates and market volatility.

## What Norges Bank said

The unanimous decision states that "inflation is too high", that it "will likely be necessary to keep the policy rate elevated for a time", and that the Committee is "prepared to raise the policy rate if needed". The krone "has appreciated and is stronger than assumed", which in isolation dampens inflation. ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/monetary-policy/Monetary-policy-meetings/2026/november-2026/))

In an interview with Nettavisen, Governor Ida Wolden Bache said that "a stronger krone makes what we import from abroad cheaper" and that the rate is set "on the basis of conditions in the Norwegian economy", while higher rates abroad tend to pull Norwegian rates up. On AI, she noted that these investments "have increasingly been financed with debt", which may have contributed to higher interest rates, and that markets must be prepared for volatility. ([Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/okonomi/sentralbanksjefen-om-kronekurs-renter-og-ki-gjeld-norges-bank/s/80-95-222675))

At the press conference the Governor added that, with inflation above target for a long time, the Bank is "particularly on guard against signs that inflationary pressures remain elevated", since persistent inflation can become harder to bring down ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/Og4dgV/sentralbanksjefen-er-saerlig-paa-vakt)). Separately, Executive Director Torbjørn Hægeland spoke on payment-system security on Thursday, without reference to monetary policy ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/Speeches/2026/2026-11-05-hageland/)). There has been no further statement from the Bank this morning; its report on the management of the foreign exchange reserves is due at 10:00.

## What economists and the market say

- **DNB Carnegie.** Kjersti Haugland: "Our starting point is that the peak has been reached", though she does not expect domestic inflation to fall enough within three years, "but Norges Bank does. We will see who is right." ([Børsen](https://borsen.dagbladet.no/nyheter/forberedt-pa-a-sette-opp-renta/85382226), [E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/p1o20V/sjefoekonom-de-holder-muligheten-aapne-for-en-heving-til))
- **Handelsbanken.** Marius Gonsholt Hov: the risk of a further hike "is reduced" and the probability "lower than a month ago". ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/p1o20V/sjefoekonom-de-holder-muligheten-aapne-for-en-heving-til))
- **SB1 Markets and NHO.** Harald Magnus Andreassen points to the krone's appreciation of about 12 per cent this year; NHO's Nejra Macić is "not entirely sure the peak has been reached". SEB's Erica Dalstø expects the rate to stay high for a longer period. ([Børsen](https://borsen.dagbladet.no/nyheter/forberedt-pa-a-sette-opp-renta/85382226))
- **Danske Bank and JP Morgan**, before the decision: Frank Jullum thinks the peak is reached but "we are balancing on a knife-edge"; Allan Monks forecast one further hike in the first quarter. ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/eG3QBa/tror-rentetoppen-er-naadd-balanserer-paa-en-knivsegg))
- **International.** Nomura expects no change in 2026–2027 and a first cut only from 2028; Commerzbank notes the market is "far from convinced" of a December hike. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/norwegian-krone-gradual-disinflation-delays-rate-cuts-nomura-202611051200), [Tradingpedia](https://www.tradingpedia.com/2026/11/05/krone-traders-focus-on-norges-bank-energy-markets/))

## The krone and rates

EUR/NOK has risen to 10.48 this morning (+0.4 per cent) and USD/NOK to 9.37 (+0.8 per cent), against 10.32 on Monday: the global sell-off in equities has outweighed both the hawkish wording and Brent near 100 dollars. At Thursday's fixes the 3-month bill yielded 4.72 per cent, the 3-year bond 4.60 per cent and the 10-year bond 4.31 per cent, the last two down 4 and 6 basis points on the day. The import-weighted index I-44, the Bank's own measure of the krone, was 107.76 at Thursday's fix, 0.5 per cent weaker on the day. A further weakening of the krone is the channel most economists identify for a December hike.
