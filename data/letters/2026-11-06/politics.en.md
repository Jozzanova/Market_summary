# Politics letter, Friday 6 November 2026

**Summary.** The political development with the largest market effect this week was the outcome of the US midterm elections on Tuesday, in which the Democratic Party won control of both chambers of Congress. Markets have read the result as raising the probability of federal regulation of AI data centres, after Senator Sanders called for a national moratorium, and the repricing on Thursday was concentrated in semiconductors and AI-related equities. In the Middle East, hopes of a US–Iran agreement that had cut oil prices by about 8 per cent on Wednesday gave way to renewed attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz, and oil recovered part of the fall. The sell-off in AI-related equities spread to Asia this morning. In Europe, the ECB faces a change of leadership in February.

## United States

- **Congress.** The Democratic sweep gives the party control of the legislative agenda from January. The immediate market focus is on energy use and permitting for data centres; the sell-off in Nvidia, Broadcom, Micron and AMD on Thursday, and in copper, reflects the expectation that construction of AI capacity could be slowed by regulation. The composition of committees and the legislative calendar are not yet known. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-5-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-plunge-chip-stocks-melt-down-193726491.html))
- **Reactions.** President Trump blamed Democrats for Thursday's market losses in a post on Truth Social. In Texas, Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa said data-centre construction should pause until protections are in place, an example of the state-level channel for the same concern. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **Monetary policy and the election.** The dollar has been described as comparatively soft since the result. The Federal Reserve's December meeting is the first after the election; two members dissented in favour of a hike in October.

## Middle East and energy

- Oil fell about 8 per cent on Wednesday on reports of progress towards a US–Iran agreement and rose 3 to 4 per cent on Thursday after renewed attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz. Brent, at about 98 to 99 dollars, remains well below last week's peak of 117 dollars but far above the levels of the summer. Iran's Revolutionary Guard reported further tanker fires in the strait on Thursday, with no claim of responsibility, while President Trump warned of a possible "heavy hit" on Iran and confirmed a limited strike near Qeshm Island; Rapidan Energy put the odds of renewed US military action at 80 per cent. These reports could not be independently verified. Brent is near 100 dollars this morning. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/iran-says-tankers-struck-in-hormuz-amid-fears-of-new-us-attack), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/middle-east-conflict-trump-warns-of-heavy-hit-as-iran-says-americans-voted-no-to-war-1.500735100), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)) The energy price is the main channel from geopolitics to inflation and to the central bank decisions in December. ([Kitco](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-11-05/gold-holds-gains-silver-slips-wall-street-plunges-fed-hike-bets-ease-kitco))

## Asia

- The repricing of AI-related equities spread to Japan, South Korea and Taiwan this morning; the KOSPI's fall of as much as 8.6 per cent triggered a 20-minute trading halt. The political channel is the same: expectations of US regulation of data centres, which would reduce demand for Asian chips and memory. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))

## Europe

- **ECB leadership.** President Lagarde announced on 29 October that she will leave office on 7 February 2027. The succession will be decided by the euro-area heads of government; the December and February meetings will be held under a departing president. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/29/ecb-holds-rates-at-25-as-lagarde-announces-early-exit-with-inflation-near-double-the-targe))
- **Defence.** Rheinmetall cut its 2026 sales forecast and fell about 17 per cent, a reminder that the European defence procurement cycle is uneven despite the political commitments to higher spending. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tech-drag-defence-weakness-weigh-164047028.html))

## Norway

- The sale by BP of about 5 per cent of Aker BP, at a 12 per cent discount, was the largest single transaction on Oslo Børs on Thursday and weighed on the energy sector. Norges Bank's hold, with a readiness to raise rates if the inflation outlook warrants it, keeps monetary policy in focus ahead of the December report. ([E24](https://e24.no/bors/nyheter/a/306142?pinnedEntry=306142), [Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/Press-releases/2026/2026-11-05-rate/))

## Dates to watch

- 9 December: Federal Reserve decision, the first under the new congressional majority.
- 16 to 18 December: Riksbank, Norges Bank, Bank of England, ECB and Bank of Japan decisions.
- 7 February 2027: President Lagarde leaves the ECB.
