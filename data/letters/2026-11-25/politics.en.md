# Politics, Wednesday 25 November 2026

**Trade policy returned as a market factor on Tuesday: according to AP, Washington is about to announce new tariffs on China, the European Union and fourteen other economies, and a US jury convicted Huawei on ten counts, adding to the strain in US–China relations. In the Gulf, renewed attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz supported oil prices. In Norway, the deadline for the 2027 budget expires this morning without an agreement so far.**

## United States

- **New tariffs under Section 301.** The US Trade Representative is expected to publish "in the coming days" the results of its investigations into excess manufacturing capacity, and to impose tariffs on China, the European Union and fourteen other economies; the rates, product coverage and effective date are not yet known, and the White House did not comment ([AP](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/24/us-ready-to-slap-tariffs-on-china-eu-and-others-over-subsidized-production-ap-sources-say/)). The duties would come on top of the 10 to 12.5 per cent tariffs announced earlier this year on 60 trading partners over forced-labour enforcement, and are meant to replace the tariffs from April 2025 that the Supreme Court struck down in February. *Market channel:* higher import prices in the United States would add to inflation in the short run and support the case for Fed tightening, while European and Asian exporters face lower demand; chip and data-centre shares fell on the reports ([AP](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/investing/market-outlook/2026/11/24/tech-stocks-wane-a-day-after-a-huang-rally-as-anthropic-tumbles-below-its-initial-offering-price/)).
- **Huawei convicted.** A federal jury in Brooklyn found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including conspiracy to steal trade secrets, bank fraud and doing business in sanctioned countries such as Iran, and acquitted it of racketeering conspiracy and conspiracy to obstruct justice; the company will appeal, sentencing is set for next September, and Chinese officials called the case "economic bullying" ([AP](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2026/11/24/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-is-guilty-of-conspiracy-to-steal-trade-secrets-us-jury-finds/)). *Market channel:* limited directly, but together with the tariff plans and the chip export curbs it raises the risk of Chinese countermeasures.
- **Chip export curbs.** The Commerce Department's rule of 16 November requires licences for advanced AI chips bought by data centres in several Southeast Asian countries and bars them from renting significant computing power to Chinese companies without approval ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-us-ai-chip-export-controls-data-centers-.html)). The effects on regional data-centre projects are still being assessed and weighed on Asian chip shares this morning.

## Middle East and energy

- **Hormuz.** Reports of further attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz lifted Brent 2.3 per cent on Tuesday. President Trump has extended the deadline for resuming attacks on Iran until after the New Year if no deal is reached, and Iraq has rejected an OPEC+-commissioned assessment of its production capacity ahead of the OPEC+ meeting this weekend ([Bloomberg](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/oil_climbs_despite_growing_oversupply_fears-24-nov-2026-185160-article/)). *Market channel:* the risk premium in oil depends on whether the attacks disrupt flows; so far the IEA's expected surplus has kept prices well below the October peak.

## Europe

- **France.** ECB Governing Council member Olaf Sleijpen said that problems with France's public finances "need to be fixed in Paris primarily" ([Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/ecb-policy-direction-clear-december-150810000.html)), a reminder that the ECB is reluctant to be seen as a backstop. The spread between euro-area all-issuer and AAA 10-year yields was 0.61 percentage point at Monday's fix, little changed.

## Norway

- **The 2027 budget.** The Storting's finance committee refused to extend the deadline, so the Labour Party and its four budget partners must agree before 10:00 today; corrections are due Thursday and the final recommendation on Friday ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nyheter/ingen-utsatt-frist-for-de-rodgronne_-ma-avklare-innen-onsdag-1.18087741)). The main obstacle is the Centre Party's demand for lower fuel taxes against the Green Party's demands on emissions ([VG](https://www.vg.no/nyheter/i/lMAxKe/budsjettforhandling-fire-dager-paa-overtid)). On Tuesday evening, Bjørn Arild Gram of the Centre Party said "vi er ikke i mål" ([TV 2](https://www.tv2.no/direkte/jpybz/siste-nytt/6b05e2e89a8c1c8fd7b90d88/budsjettforhandlingene-fortsetter-vi-er-ikke-i-mal)). *Market channel:* the sums at stake are small relative to the budget, so the effect on the fiscal stance and on interest rates is likely to be minor.
