# Key points, Wednesday 25 November 2026

**The recovery in AI shares lasted one day: semiconductors gave back most of Monday's gain and the selling has spread to Asian chip makers this morning, while shares outside technology held up. Reports of new US tariffs added to the caution, yields edged down and a Fed hike on 9 December remains almost fully priced ahead of today's PCE figures.**

**Equities.** The S&P 500 fell 0.50 per cent and the Nasdaq 1.48 per cent, while the Dow rose 0.85 per cent; the semiconductor index SOX fell 7.88 per cent, Nvidia 5.9 per cent and Anthropic 6.5 per cent, to below its offering price ([AP](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/investing/market-outlook/2026/11/24/tech-stocks-wane-a-day-after-a-huang-rally-as-anthropic-tumbles-below-its-initial-offering-price/)). STOXX Europe 600 fell 0.66 per cent, with Sanofi up 9.7 per cent on its purchase of Insmed ([Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/world-indices/articles/europes-stoxx-600-subdued-ai-082727732.html)), and Oslo Børs fell 0.87 per cent, where Tomra fell 8.8 per cent after lowering its margin target ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/3BJ8jX/tomra-faller-paa-oslo-boers)). This morning KOSPI is down 6.30 per cent and the Nikkei 2.86 per cent, led by Samsung, SK hynix and SoftBank ([AP](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/asian-stocks-mostly-decline-ai-040526833.html)).

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield fell 1.4 basis points to 4.90 per cent and the 5-year 1.6 basis points to 4.71 per cent. Fed funds futures imply a Fed hike of 23.9 basis points on 9 December, against 25.6 on Tuesday. The German 10-year fell 4 basis points to 3.33 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year (live) 6.5 basis points to 4.16 per cent.

**Currencies.** EUR/USD is 1.0947 and USD/JPY 159.37. The krone weakened slightly: EUR/NOK rose 0.32 per cent to 10.761, USD/NOK is 9.834 and I-44 rose 0.05 per cent to 111.04, probably as risk appetite declined.

**Energy.** The January Brent contract rose 2.3 per cent to 91.40 dollars on Tuesday after new attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, while expectations of a supply surplus limited the gain ([Bloomberg](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/oil_climbs_despite_growing_oversupply_fears-24-nov-2026-185160-article/)); the front-month price is 87.49 dollars this morning. TTF gas rose 2.15 per cent to 91.67 euros.

**Drivers.** AP reports that Washington will within days impose Section 301 tariffs on China, the EU and fourteen other economies, with rates not yet known ([AP](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/24/us-ready-to-slap-tariffs-on-china-eu-and-others-over-subsidized-production-ap-sources-say/)). A US jury convicted Huawei on ten counts ([AP](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2026/11/24/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-is-guilty-of-conspiracy-to-steal-trade-secrets-us-jury-finds/)). Consumer confidence rose to 103.3, well above expectations ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/treasury-yields-bonds-inflation.html)). The ECB's Olaf Sleijpen said the direction for December is "clear" ([Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/ecb-policy-direction-clear-december-150810000.html)). Australian CPI rose 4.9 per cent and the trimmed mean 3.8 per cent ([ABS](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/consumer-price-index-australia/latest-release)), and Japanese services producer prices 4.2 per cent ([Reuters](https://wkzo.com/2026/11/24/japans-corporate-services-inflation-spikes-in-october/)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.84 percentage points at twelve months in government rates and about 0.83 percentage point at ten years against Germany. Household credit growth was unchanged at 4.5 per cent in October ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/bank-og-finansmarked/finansielle-indikatorer/statistikk/kredittindikator/artikler/innenlandsk-gjeld)). No new comment on Norges Bank was found; the economists quoted expect a hold on 17 December. The labour force survey is due at 08:00, and the budget parties must agree by 10:00 ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nyheter/ingen-utsatt-frist-for-de-rodgronne_-ma-avklare-innen-onsdag-1.18087741)).
