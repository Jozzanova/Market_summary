# Key points, Thursday 12 November 2026

**Equities.** US equities rose on Wednesday after strong CoreWeave results revived the AI trade: the S&P 500 gained 1.31 per cent to 7,619, the Nasdaq 1.60 per cent and the semiconductor index SOX 5.66 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/r8LBlw/wall-street-aapner-opp)). STOXX Europe 600 rose 0.72 per cent and Oslo Børs 1.21 per cent to 2,106.5, led by Equinor and Frontline. After the close Nvidia fell almost 9 per cent on reports that its board may remove Jensen Huang ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/a93307a7-e2cd-4abd-bd89-c38832d8c40e)). In Asia this morning the Nikkei is down 0.24 per cent, the Hang Seng 0.92 per cent and the KOSPI 0.42 per cent.

**Government bonds.** Cash Treasuries were closed for Veterans Day; on reopening in Asia long bonds gained on demand for safe assets ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/long-us-bonds-reopen-with-gains-as-risk-sentiment-sours-again)), with the 10-year near 5.00 per cent. The latest official 2-year fix is 4.56 per cent (9 November). Tuesday's fixes put the euro AAA 10-year at 3.33 per cent (+5.9 basis points) and the Norwegian 10-year at 4.32 per cent (+3.9). Futures price 26 basis points of Fed tightening on 9 December.

**Currencies.** The dollar reached a 19-month high on Fed hike expectations ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/euro-falls-to-18-month-low-against-dollar-on-fed-rate-hike-bets-02d386c5)); the index is 102.98, EUR/USD 1.108 (−0.8 per cent) and USD/JPY 159.75. EUR/NOK fell 0.16 per cent to 10.599, while USD/NOK rose 0.66 per cent to 9.567 and I-44 0.13 per cent to 109.71: the dollar's strength weighed on the krone, while higher oil on Wednesday supported it against the euro.

**Energy.** Brent is 91.3 dollars, down 1.6 per cent this morning after a 1.4 per cent rise on Wednesday, on hopes for Ukraine and Iran diplomacy, higher OPEC+ output and a reported rise in US stocks ([Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/oil-prices-drop-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure/)). Dated Brent was 107.0 dollars on 3 November, well above the front contract. TTF rose 2.3 per cent to 86.7 euros.

**Drivers.** CoreWeave's results and demand for the Anthropic listing ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/639de881-6017-454f-abfa-cfb191d1e7cd)); the Nvidia leadership crisis, now a national security question in Washington ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/senators-ask-trump-administration-to-preserve-nvidia-documents)) and a strain on the Trump–Xi summit ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-fallout-puts-trade-truce-to-the-test-as-xi-welcomes-trump)); Fed hike expectations after Tuesday's inflation report; and in Japan a dovish board member's call for caution alongside faster producer price growth ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/japan-producer-prices-pick-up-backing-case-for-another-boj-hike)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.87 points at twelve months in government rates (6 basis points narrower) and 0.99 points at ten years. No new economist comments on the rate path were published; those quoted after Tuesday's CPI expect a hold in December, and the last reported pricing is about 35 per cent for a hike. Norges Bank's Financial Stability Report was published on Wednesday. SSB's investment survey is due at 08:00.
