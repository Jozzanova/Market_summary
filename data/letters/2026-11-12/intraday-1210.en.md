# Intraday update, Thursday 12 November 2026, as of 12:10 Oslo time

**Europe has risen modestly as investors rotated away from AI shares, while the Nvidia leadership question has pushed US futures lower and kept the dollar and Treasury yields firm. The krone is slightly stronger against the euro and weaker against the dollar. Oil is lower. Statistics Norway cut its estimate of oil and gas investment for 2026.**

## Equities
- **Europe is higher, led by Germany.** STOXX Europe 600 is up 0.36 per cent, the DAX 0.77 per cent and the FTSE 100 0.30 per cent. The tech-light region has benefited from the rotation away from AI shares ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/nikkei-falls-0-2-dragged-by-electronics-stocks-0da84013)). Oslo Børs is flat, down 0.04 per cent.
- **Nvidia is the main risk for the US open.** The shares were down almost 6 per cent before the open as the board considers Jensen Huang's future ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/a93307a7-e2cd-4abd-bd89-c38832d8c40e)), and the stock has dragged US futures lower ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/nvidia-leadership-scandal-drags-on-u-s-stock-futures-pressures-ai-trade-e7f6e5b8)). The VIX is 18.3.

## Rates and currencies
- **Treasury yields are higher and European yields lower**, with the dollar elevated on expectations of further Fed rate rises ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/treasury-yields-rise-as-fed-rate-hike-expectations-remain-strong-401fed89)). The 10-year Treasury yield was 5.00 per cent at Wednesday's close.
- **The dollar is firmer.** EUR/USD is 1.1100 (down 0.6 per cent), USD/JPY 159.56 and the dollar index 102.82.
- **The krone is mixed.** EUR/NOK is 10.586, 0.3 per cent lower than at the last close, while USD/NOK has risen 0.4 per cent to 9.540. The Norwegian 10-year yield was 4.30 per cent on Wednesday.

## Commodities
- **Oil is lower.** Brent is 91.88 dollars (down 0.9 per cent), and the January contract is about 6 dollars below a week ago. Dutch TTF gas is up 3.9 per cent to 90.0 euros per megawatt-hour. Gold is down 1.0 per cent at 3,960 dollars.

## Data and central banks
- **Norway: oil investment is lower.** Statistics Norway expects 266 billion kroner in 2026, nine billion less than in its previous survey, and 237 billion in 2027 ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/Qrlp88/jekker-ned-aarets-oljeinvesteringer)). The first sets a slightly weaker tone for domestic activity; it does not change the rate outlook.
- **UK growth was strong.** GDP rose 0.7 per cent in the third quarter, the strongest in more than two years ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/94f3ad1c-a186-4893-ae97-5f129690d121)), but it will not by itself move the Bank of England in December ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/f92f05b4-0d1a-4bdb-8759-4d71f1cf58af)). I have not verified the consensus.
