# Norges Bank, Thursday 12 November 2026

**Summary.** No new Norwegian data and no new economist comments on the rate path were published in the last 24 hours, so the standing expectation is unchanged: economists quoted after Tuesday's CPI expect the policy rate to stay at 4.50 per cent in December, and the latest reported market pricing is about 35 per cent for a hike. Norges Bank published its Financial Stability Report on Wednesday. The krone was broadly stable against the euro but weaker against the dollar, as Fed hike expectations lifted the dollar.

## New data

No new Norwegian statistics were released on Wednesday. At 08:00 today Statistics Norway publishes the investment survey for the fourth quarter, which gives the first estimate of petroleum investment in 2027 and is an important input for the outlook for the mainland economy, together with retail trade and job vacancies for the third quarter ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/kalender)). Tuesday's CPI showed inflation of 3.6 per cent and CPI-ATE of 2.7 per cent, against a Bloomberg consensus of 3.7 and 2.7 ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)).

**Norges Bank.** The Financial Stability Report judged the system resilient but the risk of serious events higher; the countercyclical buffer is unchanged at 2.5 per cent ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/Press-releases/2026/2026-11-11-fsr2/)).

## What others say

No economist was quoted on the rate path in the last 24 hours. The standing views:

- **Marius Gonsholt Hov, Handelsbanken:** the October CPI "endrer ikke noe på renteforventningene nå" (does not change rate expectations now); he expects the rate to be held this year ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)).
- **Kyrre M. Knudsen, SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge:** expects a hold in December and a signal that the peak has been reached ([Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/okonomi/alt-blir-dyrere-kpi-pa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober/s/80-95-235224)).
- **Finanstilsynet** said on Wednesday that many depositors could obtain a better rate by switching bank and proposed measures to strengthen consumer protection ([E24](https://e24.no/privatoekonomi/i/zlb1Aq/finanstilsynet-forbrukervernet-hos-bankene-boer-styrkes)). The proposals bear on pass-through of the policy rate to deposit rates rather than on the rate path.

None of the economists quoted expects a hike in December. Abroad, the case for tightening has strengthened: the Fed is fully priced to hike on 9 December and the ECB and Bank of England are priced at about 70 per cent. Higher foreign rates narrow the krone's rate advantage and pull in the direction of a firmer Norwegian path, while core inflation on Norges Bank's projection pulls against it.

## The krone and rates

**Exchange rates.** EUR/NOK is 10.599, 0.16 per cent lower than on Wednesday, while USD/NOK rose 0.66 per cent to 9.567. I-44 rose 0.13 per cent to 109.71 at Wednesday's fix and is 2.35 per cent weaker on the week.

**Why.** The krone weakened against the dollar but not against the euro. That is consistent with a broadly stronger dollar on Fed hike expectations ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/central-banking/euro-falls-to-18-month-low-against-dollar-on-fed-rate-hike-bets-02d386c5)), while the 1.4 per cent rise in Brent on Wednesday supported the krone against the euro; no source attributes the move. Brent's fall this morning to 91.3 dollars works the other way.

**Differentials.**
- Against the euro area: 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate; 1.87 points at twelve months in government rates (the Norwegian 12-month bill against the euro AAA 1-year rate, a stand-in for the swap differential), 6 basis points narrower as the euro rate rose; 0.99 points at ten years (Tuesday's fixes).
- Against the United States: 0.41 points at twelve months in government rates.
