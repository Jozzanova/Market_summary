# Politics, Thursday 12 November 2026

**The political news of the day bears on markets mainly through two channels: the Nvidia affair, which has become a national security question in Washington and now overshadows the US–China summit, and the diplomacy around Iran and Ukraine, which has lowered the risk premium in oil. Democrats widened their Senate majority, and Germany's coalition cleared the way for its 2027 budget.**

## United States

**Nvidia and China.** Two Democratic senators asked the administration to preserve all records on exports of Nvidia's advanced chips to China ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/senators-ask-trump-administration-to-preserve-nvidia-documents)), and Huang's standing in Washington has weakened after reports of an affair with a suspected Chinese spy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/9ca8ba83-5a3c-409e-96d9-abee1323b688)). The market channel is twofold: leadership risk at the largest company in the S&P 500, and the risk of tighter export controls on AI chips.

**Congress.** Democrats added a Senate seat in Mississippi, where Scott Colom defeated Senator Hyde-Smith in a state Trump won by more than 22 points ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/democrat-colom-wins-mississippi-senate-race-cnn-projects)). Former congressman Kevin Brady expects government funding to be extended in the lame-duck session rather than a shutdown ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-11/brady-says-democrats-control-lame-duck-funding-timing-video)). Analysts cite the midterm outcome, alongside AI spending, as a reason for cutting 2027 earnings forecasts ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/ai-malaise-midterms-cloud-2027-profits-after-blowout-quarter)).

## Asia

Xi Jinping's third summit of the year with Donald Trump is under pressure from the Nvidia allegations before the talks begin ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-fallout-puts-trade-truce-to-the-test-as-xi-welcomes-trump)). A breakdown in the trade truce would affect tariffs and chip export rules, the two channels most relevant to equities. In Indonesia the vice president resigned ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/jokowi-son-gibran-rakabuming-raka-resigns-indonesia-vice-president-prabowo)), and the rupiah fell to a record low ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/idr-usd-indonesian-rupiah-drops-to-record-amid-msci-concerns-oil-risks)).

## Middle East, Ukraine and energy

Vice President Vance has emerged as the lead US negotiator with Iran and is considering direct talks with a hard-liner ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/vance-considers-direct-talks-with-iran-hardliner-in-push-to-end-war-0c2e3ea4)); Treasury Secretary Bessent has been moved to a military base after threats linked to Iran ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/politics/national-security/bessent-moved-to-military-base-after-threats-linked-to-iran-95cfced1)). Talks on Ukraine in Abu Dhabi ended with a prisoner exchange but no breakthrough ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/39254048-0d84-47b8-bb97-4b9d9e1f2b92)). Reuters cites both tracks as reasons for this morning's fall in oil ([Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/oil-prices-drop-1-hopes-iran-ukraine-peace-talks-pressure/)); Iran's position that enriched material will not leave the country suggests the gap remains wide.

## Europe

Germany's coalition rolled back planned welfare cuts and delayed new taxes, clearing the way for the 2027 budget ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/germany-delays-new-taxes-power-tax-cut-rolls-back-welfare-cuts)). In the United Kingdom, Chancellor Healey said the Treasury is discussing "worst-case" energy support for households and that an end to the Iran war would be the biggest boost to the economy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/22798c90-e999-4564-924a-4f04343cac3d)); such support would add to borrowing.

## Norway

Finanstilsynet said consumer protection in banking is too weak and that many depositors could obtain a better rate by switching bank; it will study a duty to advise and a symmetry principle for rate changes ([E24](https://e24.no/privatoekonomi/i/zlb1Aq/finanstilsynet-forbrukervernet-hos-bankene-boer-styrkes)). The deadline set by Labour for a 2027 budget agreement is 20 November.
