## Equities
- **The US indices closed a little lower.** The Nasdaq Composite fell about 0.4 per cent ([Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/stocks-slip-as-wall-street-weighs-nvidias-ceo-crisis-stock-market-today)); in the brief the S&P 500 is down 0.35 per cent, the Dow up 0.23 per cent and the Russell 2000 down 0.52 per cent. The markets recovered well from their morning lows.
- **Nvidia fell about 4 per cent at the close** after as much as 13 per cent at the open, following Jensen Huang's resignation; Colette Kress is interim CEO. AMD and Broadcom rose more than 5 per cent each ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/11/investing/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang)). Nvidia says it will still report on 17 November.
- **Europe closed higher.** STOXX Europe 600 rose 0.24 per cent and the Euro Stoxx 50 0.23 per cent; the Oslo Børs was little changed.
