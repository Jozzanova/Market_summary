# Key points, Tuesday 24 November 2026

**Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, brought about with the US administration's support, reversed last week's sale of AI shares and gave US equities their best day since April 2025, a recovery that has spread to Asia this morning. As demand for safe assets eased, the market moved to price a Fed hike on 9 December fully, while lower oil prices kept long-dated yields steady and weakened the krone.**

**Equities.** The S&P 500 rose 4.73 per cent, the Nasdaq 6.51 per cent and the Dow 3.01 per cent; Nvidia rose 31.5 per cent and the semiconductor index SOX 14.03 per cent ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/stocks-rebound-ai-tech-selloff-nvidia-rcna610833)). STOXX Europe 600 rose 1.81 per cent and Oslo Børs 0.50 per cent, where Vår Energi fell 3.3 per cent after agreeing to buy ConocoPhillips' Norwegian business ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/z0Gj1r/vaar-energi-kjoeper-conocophillips-norske-virksomhet-i-avtale-verdt-43-milliarder-kroner)). This morning KOSPI is up 7.00 per cent, the Nikkei 3.97 per cent and Taiwan more than 9 per cent ([AP](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/feec0-asian-stocks-rally-after-wall-street-rebound-as-jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidia-s-ceo/)).

**Government bonds.** The US 2-year yield rose more than 7 basis points to 4.69 per cent and the 5-year 2.7 basis points to 4.73 per cent, while the 10-year changed little at 4.92 per cent, as investors sold havens and oil fell ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/treasury-yields-bonds-fed.html)). Fed funds futures now price a Fed hike of 25.6 basis points on 9 December, fully priced, after 22.5 basis points on Monday morning. The German 10-year fell 2 basis points to 3.37 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year (live) 5 basis points to 4.23 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 104.28, EUR/USD fell 0.54 per cent to 1.0913 and USD/JPY is 158.99. The krone weakened: EUR/NOK is 10.720, USD/NOK 9.826 and I-44 rose 0.22 per cent to 110.98, as the fall in oil outweighed higher risk appetite.

**Energy.** Brent fell about 3.6 per cent on Monday and is 86.18 dollars this morning, after President Trump signalled more time for diplomacy with Iran and traders unwound hedges against a weekend escalation that did not come ([Bloomberg](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/oil_falls_as_trump_signals_iran_talks-23-nov-2026-185152-article/)). TTF gas rose 1.45 per cent to 89.74 euros.

**Drivers.** Nvidia's board said, citing input from the US government, that Huang's return "poses no national security risk" ([Nvidia](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)). New York Fed President John Williams said he would not exclude Fed hikes at consecutive meetings ([Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fed-lower-inflation-williams-says-210546185.html)), the ECB's Gediminas Šimkus doubted that one ECB hike in December will be enough ([Reuters](https://1027wbow.com/2026/11/23/december-rate-hike-may-not-be-enough-but-not-in-a-rush-to-move-again-ecbs-simkus/)), and the flash PMIs showed slower growth with easing cost pressure in the United States and the euro area ([Reuters](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1123/1600272-euro-zone-growth-pmi/)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.84 percentage points at twelve months in government rates (Friday's fix) and about 0.86 percentage point at ten years against Germany. No new comment on Norges Bank was found; the economists quoted expect a hold on 17 December. The oil fund's fall has lowered the fiscal room, with proposed spending now 2.81 per cent of the fund ([NRK](https://nrk.no/artikkel/oljefondet-har-krympet-over-1000-milliarder-blast-bort-1533745)), and the budget talks continue today without agreement.
