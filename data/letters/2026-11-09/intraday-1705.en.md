# Intraday update, Monday 9 November 2026, as of 17:05 Oslo time

**Summary.** Equities rebounded broadly today as semiconductors recovered: the S&P 500 is up 2.1 per cent to 7,531 in the US session, the STOXX Europe 600 up 1.3 per cent and the Oslo Børs benchmark up 1.3 per cent, even though Nvidia is about 4 per cent lower on the board's investigation of its chief executive. Brent has fallen 3.6 per cent to 94.8 dollars on hopes of a US-Iran deal. The krone is weaker, with EUR/NOK up 0.9 per cent to 10.54. Tuesday's consumer prices in Norway and the United States are the next test.
