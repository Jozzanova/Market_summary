# Norges Bank letter, Monday 9 November 2026

**Summary.** There was no new communication from Norges Bank over the weekend; the latest items on its news page remain Thursday's decision to keep the policy rate at 4.50 per cent and the two speeches that day. The standing view is a hold for some time with a readiness to hike if needed. Tuesday's October CPI is the next test: economists expect CPI-ATE to fall to 2.7 per cent, and Sparebank 1 Markets says it would take a clear upside surprise to matter for the rate.

## What Norges Bank said

Nothing new since 5 November ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/)). The message stands: inflation is too high, it "will likely be necessary to keep the policy rate elevated for a time", and the Committee is "prepared to raise the policy rate if needed" ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/monetary-policy/Monetary-policy-meetings/2026/november-2026/)). The Financial Stability Report on Wednesday is the next scheduled publication.

## What economists and the market say

- Harald Magnus Andreassen, chief economist at Sparebank 1 Markets, said CPI would have to come in well above expectations before it matters for Norges Bank, and he does not expect a hike in 2026. Bloomberg's consensus for October is 3.7 per cent for CPI and 2.7 per cent for CPI-ATE ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/qOnMOg/venter-paa-signaler-kan-snu-opp-ned-paa-hele-regnestykket)).
- Among the international notes, Nomura expects no further rate changes in 2026–2027 as gradual disinflation delays cuts ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/norwegian-krone-gradual-disinflation-delays-rate-cuts-nomura-202611051200)), while Commerzbank's note, headlined "policy steady as oil risks dominate", sees oil as the main driver of the krone; the full text could not be accessed ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/norwegian-krone-policy-steady-as-oil-risks-dominate-commerzbank-202611061236)).
- No new comments from DNB Carnegie, Nordea, Handelsbanken, SEB, Danske or Swedbank on the rate path were found for the weekend.

## The krone and rates

EUR/NOK is at 10.54 this morning, around Friday's close and 2.5 per cent higher than a week ago; the I-44 index ended Friday at 108.79, 2.0 per cent weaker over the week. Part of the krone's strength since September, which Norges Bank cited as dampening inflation, has thus been reversed, but the index is still 9.2 per cent stronger this year. At Thursday's fixes the 3-year government yield was 4.60 per cent and the 10-year 4.31 per cent, both lower over the week, so the bond market has not responded to the weaker krone by pricing a firmer path.
