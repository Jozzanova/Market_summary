# Politics letter, Monday 9 November 2026

**Summary.** The weekend's political news turned on the aftermath of the US midterms and the Iran talks. President Trump conceded that his deportation policy and the Iran war likely cost Republicans votes and floated an immigration deal, and he said renewed strikes on Iran were "highly unlikely" if talks progress, ahead of a meeting with Qatar's prime minister today. China formalised a two-month extension of its tariff truce with the United States. In Europe, finance ministers discuss the ECB succession and the UK prime minister heads to a European Political Community meeting in Dublin.

## United States

- **After the midterms.** Trump said his administration may have been "too tough" on deportations and floated an amnesty deal, conceding that voters were turned off by the Iran war and "bad messaging" on the economy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/c82ac9a2-617e-400a-a5b7-e8d6674a80d4), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/trump-says-tough-deporation-policy-likely-hurt-gop-in-midterms)). He also said he was "only kidding" when he offered the press secretary job to UFC chief Dana White ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-08/trump-says-asked-ufc-s-white-to-be-his-press-secretary-as-a-joke)). The market channel is AI investment: UBS chief executive Sergio Ermotti said the Democratic sweep could slow the build-out of data centres ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-09/ubs-ceo-midterms-creating-uncertainty-for-ai-buildout-video)), and the FT reports that analysts see a tougher line on data-centre construction giving China a window to catch up ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/ff0e9974-b855-4b9a-a8c9-0782e7af16ff)).
- **Trade with China.** Beijing will keep its additional 24 per cent tariff on US goods suspended until January while retaining a 10 per cent rate ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-extends-pause-on-24-us-tariffs-to-january-keeps-10-rate)), and Washington is pressing China to follow through on farm purchases ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/soybeans-rise-as-us-presses-china-to-boost-farm-goods-purchases)).

## Middle East and energy

Trump told CNN that renewed attacks on Iran are "highly unlikely" if talks show progress, while noting a third aircraft carrier group in the region; Qatar is circulating a framework document that both sides are amending ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/08/politics/trump-interview-on-iran)). On the ground, Iran's navy said a tanker caught fire after hitting mines east of the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, and US Central Command said it disabled a vessel trying to breach its blockade ([Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/irans-army-says-violating-oil-tanker-hit-mines-in-hormuz-strait)). Brent is near 98 dollars, 16 per cent lower than a week ago.
