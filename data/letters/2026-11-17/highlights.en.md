# Key points, Tuesday 17 November 2026

**Equities.** The fall in AI and semiconductor shares broadened on Monday: the S&P 500 fell 1.92 per cent, the Nasdaq 2.49 per cent and the Dow 1.41 per cent, while SOX fell 5.28 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Nvidia fell 4.1 per cent ahead of tonight's results, Micron 7.6 per cent and AMD 5.6 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/xJPJWn/markant-tek-fall-paa-wall-street-nvidia-fortsetter-nedgangen)). STOXX Europe 600 fell 0.28 per cent ([Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/european-stocks-ease-as-tech-and-miners-drag-ce785edfdd89ff2c)) and Oslo Børs 0.41 per cent, with Equinor down 2.94 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/bors/nyheter/a/306258)). This morning KOSPI is down 1.8 per cent, the Nikkei 1.0 per cent and the Hang Seng 0.8 per cent ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449311/asia-shares-slide-on-tech-weakness-ahead-of-nvidia-earnings)).

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield fell 3 basis points to 5.12 per cent and the 5-year 5 basis points to 4.93 per cent, partly reversing last week's rise. Fed funds futures price a Fed hike of about 24 basis points on 9 December and a further 19 basis points by 27 January, little changed. The euro-area AAA 10-year was 3.41 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year 4.36 per cent at Friday's fix; the German 10-year rose 2 basis points to 3.45 per cent on Monday.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.52, after touching its highest since April 2025 overnight; EUR/USD is 1.1008 and USD/JPY 159.15 ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449311/asia-shares-slide-on-tech-weakness-ahead-of-nvidia-earnings)). The krone has weakened: EUR/NOK is 10.617, 0.7 per cent above Monday's close, USD/NOK 9.646, and I-44 was 109.55 at Monday's fix, as oil and equities fell.

**Energy.** Brent fell 3.6 per cent on Monday to 92.44 dollars and is 91.74 dollars this morning, as the IEA's estimate of a supply surplus and high Saudi output outweighed the tensions with Iran ([Bloomberg](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/oil_retreats_as_traders_weigh_supply_glut-16-nov-2026-185096-article/)). TTF gas fell 6.8 per cent to 84.75 euros.

**Drivers.** New US licence requirements for advanced chips sent to data centres serving Chinese companies ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6222897-bis-limits-remote-computing-exports/amp/)) added to the uncertainty over Nvidia; Vice President Vance said the conflict with Iran must not become "even a two-year commitment" ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/jd-vance/vance-says-no-daylight-trump-iran-war-rcna609681)); Cleveland Fed president Beth Hammack favours small, steady hikes but sees a risk of larger ones ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/fed-s-hammack-reiterates-preference-for-small-incremental-hikes)); RBA governor Michele Bullock said inflation risks remain skewed to the upside ([RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/speeches/2026/sp-gov-2026-11-17.html)); Chinese activity data for October were weaker than expected ([Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/articles/chinas-factory-output-retail-sales-020559490.html)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.82 percentage points at twelve months in government rates and 0.95 percentage points at ten years. No economist published a new view on Norges Bank on Monday; those quoted expect a hold on 17 December, and the last reported pricing is about 35 per cent for a hike. The trade surplus rose to 121.4 billion kroner in October, the highest since 2022, on gas exports ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/utenriksokonomi/utenrikshandel/statistikk/utenrikshandel-med-varer/artikler/hoyeste-handelsoverskudd-siden-2022)).
