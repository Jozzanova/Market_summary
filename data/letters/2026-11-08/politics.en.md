# Politics, Sunday 8 November 2026

**Summary.** The aftermath of the US midterms dominated the weekend. President Trump signalled a combative approach towards the incoming Democratic majority, while several Senate Republicans pushed to delay their leadership election. There was no decision on whether to resume major strikes on Iran, and Tehran repeated that the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until its conditions are met. In Israel, opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot was given the first chance to form a government, and New Zealand's election produced no clear result.

## United States

- **Washington after the midterms.** Trump said he had ordered the Justice Department to act after Democrats threatened to investigate his administration and Republican donors ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/trump-threatens-democrats-over-their-plans-to-probe-his-team)), and asked UFC chief Dana White to become press secretary, which the FT describes as a signal of a combative approach ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/52c3c7ac-f0ee-4b90-a2c8-68e76d120a79)). Several Senate Republicans want next week's leadership election delayed ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/several-senate-republicans-call-for-delaying-leadership-election)). The market channel is gridlock in fiscal and regulatory policy, which investors have linked to last week's sell-off in AI stocks.
- **Energy permitting.** A Senate bill to fast-track energy and infrastructure approvals is heading for a showdown ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/senate-plan-to-fast-track-energy-projects-headed-for-a-showdown)); it matters for data-centre power supply.

## Middle East and energy

Trump confirmed a "minor" strike on an Iranian air-defence site near Hormuz on Friday, while Qatar said its mediation "remains active". Iran's Mohsen Rezaei said the strait will not open "until the Americans fulfill our seven conditions" ([HNGN](https://www.hngn.com/articles/273722/20261107/trump-says-iran-decision-coming-soon-advisers-split.htm)). The US is also setting up a unit to disrupt Iran's overland arms routes ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/us-launches-effort-to-disrupt-land-routes-iran-uses-to-ship-arms)). In Israel, President Herzog gave Eisenkot 28 days to form a coalition that could end Benjamin Netanyahu's tenure ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/bf737648-b974-4f87-ba0b-958f2aff1163)). The channel is oil: Brent ended the week at 98 dollars, 16 per cent lower.

## Europe and Asia

- Five EU leaders called for closer EU–UK ties on clean energy ahead of a Brussels summit ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/07/europe-leaders-call-closer-eu-uk-ties-brussels-summit)). In France, Jean-Luc Mélenchon rallied 20,000 supporters, a reminder of the political risk around French fiscal policy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/5cb562ec-5493-4ff3-bd2d-99cc9cacfa73)).
- In New Zealand's election, the governing coalition holds 67 of 131 projected seats in the preliminary count, one more than a majority, with several hundred thousand special votes still to be counted ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/governing-party-leads-in-new-zealands-knife-edge-vote-early-results), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/07/world/new-zealand-election-result-uncertain-intl-hnk)).
