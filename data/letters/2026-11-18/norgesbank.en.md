# Norges Bank, Wednesday 18 November 2026

**There were no new Norwegian key figures and no new comments on Norges Bank on Tuesday. The standing view among the economists quoted is a hold on 17 December. The krone was steady, as a slightly firmer oil price and a small fall on Oslo Børs offset the global fall in equities; Thursday's Expectations Survey is the next input.**

## New data

**None on Tuesday.** Statistics Norway publishes the labour cost index for the third quarter at 08:00 today, and Norges Bank's Expectations Survey for the fourth quarter follows on Thursday. The survey will show whether the fall in inflation to 3.6 per cent in October has lowered expectations among economists, the social partners and businesses; expectations two and five years ahead are the figures that matter for the rate path.

**Data centres and power.** Statnett has stopped new grid allocations to data centres until August 2027, while eight projects with 624 megawatts go ahead ([E24](https://e24.no/naeringsliv/i/0vE68o/nscale-prosjekt-slipper-stroemfrys)). The projects add to investment demand in the coming years and to the demand for electricity, which bears on power prices and thereby CPI.

## What others say

- **No new comments.** No bank economist commented on Norges Bank on Tuesday that could be found.
- **Standing views.** After the October CPI, Kyrre M. Knudsen of SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge said he expects Norges Bank to keep the rate unchanged in December, and Harald Magnus Andreassen of SpareBank 1 Markets that inflation is on the track Norges Bank assumed ([NTB](https://www.frifagbevegelse.no/nyheter/okonomer-avlyser-renteokning-i-ar/2732723)). Marius Gonsholt Hov of Handelsbanken said a hike before Christmas would require "en ganske kraftig oppside" ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)), and Frank Jullum of Danske Bank believes the peak has been reached ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/eG3QBa/tror-rentetoppen-er-naadd-balanserer-paa-en-knivsegg)). Allan Monks of JP Morgan, alone among those quoted, expects a further hike, in the first quarter.
- **Market pricing.** The last reported figure is about 35 per cent for a Norges Bank hike on 17 December (Handelsbanken, 5 November); no newer figure was found.

## The krone and rates

**Exchange rates.** EUR/NOK is 10.590 this morning, 0.25 per cent below Tuesday's close, and USD/NOK 9.637; I-44 was 109.63 at Tuesday's fix, little changed from Monday.

**Why.** The krone held up although European equities had their largest fall since March. Brent rose slightly on Tuesday ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449308/oil-prices-caught-between-rising-mideast-exports-and-tanker-attacks)), which supports the terms of trade, and Oslo Børs fell markedly less than the European indices, which limits the currency-hedging flows that tend to weaken the krone when equity prices fall. No source attributes the move specifically.

**Differentials.** Against the euro area the differential is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.79 percentage points at twelve months in government rates (the stand-in for the swap differential), 3 basis points lower than in the previous fix, and 0.96 percentage points at ten years. The Norwegian 10-year yield (live) rose 2 basis points to 4.41 per cent on Tuesday, while the German 10-year fell 3 basis points.
