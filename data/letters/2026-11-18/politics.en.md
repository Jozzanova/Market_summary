# Politics, Wednesday 18 November 2026

**The conflict with Iran moved closer to the oil market again on Tuesday: tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran targeted Kuwait and Bahrain, and the United States struck Qeshm Island. Oil rose only slightly, which suggests the market still gives more weight to the prospect of a supply surplus. In Asia, the APEC meeting in Shenzhen opened with Xi Jinping warning against protectionism, a day before he meets President Trump and Vladimir Putin.**

## Middle East and energy

The UK Maritime Trade Operations reported that a tanker was struck by an unidentified projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday and caught fire, while Iran's navy said four oil tankers had struck mines in the eastern part of the strait over the previous 24 hours; Iran's Revolutionary Guards warned shipping against the US-backed southern route ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/iran-war-trump-hormuz-oil-.html)). The United States struck Iran's Qeshm Island after Iran targeted an American base in Bahrain ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/e79991c9-3f45-461a-9e3a-9c9f15ec029a)), and Kuwait said this morning that it was countering "hostile drone attacks" ([AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261118-stocks-drop-as-booming-nvidia-results-drowned-by-spy-scandal)). A third US aircraft carrier has arrived in the Gulf. The market channel is the risk premium in oil and gas: Brent rose only slightly on Tuesday because recovering Gulf exports and the IEA's projected surplus weigh against the attacks ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449308/oil-prices-caught-between-rising-mideast-exports-and-tanker-attacks)), and US strategic reserves are at their lowest since 1982, which limits Washington's room to dampen a new price rise.

## United States and China

- **APEC.** Xi Jinping told business leaders in Shenzhen that Asia-Pacific economies should deepen cooperation on trade and technology and warned against "rampant" protectionism, days after the new US curbs on Chinese access to advanced chips ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/xi-warns-against-rampant-protectionism-ahead-of-trump-talks)). Trump meets Xi and Putin on Thursday and also plans to meet Mexico's president Claudia Sheinbaum ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/trump-plans-to-meet-mexico-s-sheinbaum-during-stop-in-china)). The channel is tariffs, export controls and the yuan, which the central bank fixed at its strongest level since 2022.
- **Nvidia.** Former chief executive Jensen Huang has agreed to meet US lawmakers on 1 December on chips and national security ([AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261118-stocks-drop-as-booming-nvidia-results-drowned-by-spy-scandal)). The administration is also pushing to block imports of Chinese data-centre equipment, which Amazon, Alphabet and Meta oppose ([Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/wall-street-ends-lower-ahead-of-nvidia-results-focus-turns-to-anthropic-debut-ce785edcdc81fe2d)).
- **Congress.** Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican Dan Sullivan in Alaska, the last Senate race of the midterms, raising the Democratic majority to 56 of 100 seats ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/dc73d4ce-0139-4a72-9b2a-ab9803a84240)). The majority matters for the funding deadline on 11 December and for the confirmation of Fed nominees.

## Europe

Marine Le Pen's lead in the French presidential race has the EU working to agree future funding and aid for Ukraine before the election ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/le-pens-lead-in-french-election-race-has-eu-in-scramble-for-funding-9f4e2001)), and she has floated lowering the retirement age to as low as 60 ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/c7ef0eb8-c9c5-46ef-b65d-c0ceaf111717)). The channel is the French spread: fiscal proposals of this kind tend to widen it, and the euro-area periphery spread has risen over the year.

## Norway

The budget talks between Labour and the Centre Party, the Socialist Left, Red and the Greens continue towards Labour's deadline at 23:00 on Friday; no report on the state of the talks on Tuesday was found. Statnett has stopped new grid allocations to data centres until August 2027, but eight projects with 624 megawatts, including Nscale in Narvik and Google in Skien, go ahead ([E24](https://e24.no/naeringsliv/i/0vE68o/nscale-prosjekt-slipper-stroemfrys)). The channel is power demand and investment: the eight projects would nearly double data-centre capacity in Norway. Equinor reported an ongoing cyber-attack on certain IT systems, without effect on production ([Equinor](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/equinor-detected-cyber-attack-ce785edcdd8cf427)).
