# Politics, Monday 16 November 2026

**The week's political calendar is dominated by President Trump's Asia trip, which starts today and culminates in a meeting with Xi Jinping and the first trilateral meeting with Xi and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday. Officials expect no concrete agreements. In the Gulf, Iran claimed a new drone attack on a US base in Bahrain on Sunday, but the oil market has so far looked through it.**

## United States

- **The Asia trip is about implementing the trade deal with China rather than new concessions.** Trump leaves for Manila today and goes on to the APEC summit in Shenzhen; on Thursday he meets Xi bilaterally and then Xi and Putin together, a meeting US officials describe as an initial conversation focused on nuclear arms control ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-meet-putin-xi-china-week-us-officials/story?id=138128914)). China extended its suspension of an additional 24 per cent tariff on US goods by two months from 10 November ([VOA](https://www.eurasiareview.com/15112026-trump-xi-talks-to-test-progress-on-trade-si-and-strategic-stability/)). Bloomberg notes that Trump arrives weakened by the midterm defeat ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/weakened-at-home-trump-looks-to-flex-power-at-putin-xi-summit)). The market channel runs through the yuan, soybeans and rare-earth and semiconductor equities.
- **Technology controls.** The United States and nine partners, including India, Singapore and the United Kingdom, signed a pledge on Sunday to protect sensitive AI technologies and critical infrastructure ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/us-partners-agree-to-safeguard-key-ai-tech-infrastructure)).
- **Fiscal deadline.** The current stopgap funds the federal government until 11 December ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R49353)); no reports of negotiations on a new bill were found.

## Middle East and energy

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they sent six drones at a US base in Bahrain on Sunday; Bahrain said it intercepted several attacks, its first interceptions since September, and the Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi targets that Saudi Arabia has not confirmed ([Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/11/15/bahrain-iran-drone-attacks-tehran-us-base.html)). US officials estimate that Iran still has up to 2,500 missiles ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/u-s-figures-iran-still-has-up-to-2-500-missiles-countering-trump-assertions-4c6f4d67)). Brent has fallen only slightly this morning: the risk premium appears limited by the expectation of a supply surplus ([Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/oil-prices-fall-on-expectations-of-coming-supply-surplus.html)). OPEC+ decides on its 2027 framework on 29 November.

## Europe and Ukraine

- **Ukraine financing.** The European Commission will propose a 45 billion euro loan to Ukraine, borrowed against the EU budget, to member states on Wednesday ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/2c9de06b-aa86-4275-b9f3-1276f8c20994)); more common EU borrowing adds to the supply of euro-denominated safe assets.
- **Negotiations.** After US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Putin, Witkoff said "progress was made" while Kushner said "significant differences remain" ([Time](https://time.com/article/2026/11/12/ukraine-russia-peace-deal-talks-putin-zelensky-witkoff-kushner/)). Wheat fell on hopes that talks could free up Black Sea shipments ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-16/wheat-falls-on-optimism-trilateral-talks-will-free-up-black-sea)).

## Norway

The Supreme Court's ruling on Friday that the approvals of Breidablikk, Tyrving and Yggdrasil are invalid has entered the budget talks: MDG demands an immediate halt to production on the fields ([NTB](https://www.adressa.no/nyheter/innenriks/i/z0GgVw/mdg-krever-full-oljestans-etter-klimadom)), while the government attorney Fredrik Sejersted says the permits still apply to the companies ([NTB](https://www.adressa.no/nyheter/innenriks/i/5306Em/regjeringsadvokaten-om-klimadom-betyr-ikke-at-tillatelsene-bortfaller)). Aker BP says production on Tyrving continues ([Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/nyheter/aker-bp-etter-klimadom-produksjonen-fortsetter/s/80-95-247307)). No progress in the budget talks was reported over the weekend; Labour's deadline is Friday ([NRK](https://www.nrk.no/nyheter/statsbudsjettet-2027-1.18034953)).
