# Norges Bank, Monday 16 November 2026

**No new Norwegian key figures were published over Friday and the weekend, and no bank economist published a new view on Norges Bank that could be found. The standing expectation is a hold at 4.50 per cent on 17 December. The main domestic news is the Supreme Court ruling on three oil fields, which bears on the long-term outlook for petroleum investment rather than on the rate path, and has become an issue in the budget talks.**

## New data

None since Friday. External trade in goods for October is published at 08:00 today; in September the trade surplus was 105 billion kroner, lifted by the conflict in the Middle East ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/utenriksokonomi/utenrikshandel/statistikk/utenrikshandel-med-varer)). The latest labour market figure points to a labour market that is no longer easing: job vacancies rose 2.9 per cent in the third quarter to 89,500, and the vacancy rate rose to 2.8 per cent ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/arbeid-og-lonn/sysselsetting/statistikk/ledige-stillingar/artikler/nesten-90-000-ledige-stillingar)). A tighter labour market would add to wage pressure.

The Supreme Court ruled 6–5 on Friday that the approvals of Breidablikk, Tyrving and Yggdrasil must be reassessed ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/M5gz2r/klimasoeksmaalet-tillatelser-maa-behandles-paa-nytt)). Aker BP says production on Tyrving continues and still aims to start Yggdrasil in 2027 ([Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/nyheter/aker-bp-etter-klimadom-produksjonen-fortsetter/s/80-95-247307)).

## What others say

- **Handelsbanken** sees the peak as reached: "in our view, Norges Bank has peaked, and we expect the first cut to 4.25 percent by the third quarter of next year", and it describes the balance of risks for the krone as having "turned from tailwind to headwind" ([Pound Sterling Live](https://www.poundsterlinglive.com/news/23869-pound-to-krone-handelsbanken-krone-rally-over), 11 November).
- **Other bank economists** have not changed their view since the October CPI: Kyrre M. Knudsen of SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge expects a hold and a signal that the peak has been reached ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)).
- **Market pricing:** the latest reported figure remains about 35 per cent for a December hike (5 November); Handelsbanken's later note says money markets price "little chance" of a hike. No newer figure was found.

None of the economists quoted expects a hike in December. The argument for tightening rests on tightening abroad: Fed and Bank of England officials continue to argue for further hikes, which narrows the differential and, all else equal, weakens the krone. Thursday's Expectations Survey will show whether inflation expectations have moved further from the target.

## The krone and rates

**Exchange rates.** EUR/NOK is 10.553 this morning, 0.14 per cent below Friday's close, and USD/NOK 9.576; I-44 was 109.34 at Friday's fix, 0.5 per cent weaker over the week.

**Why.** Over the week the krone weakened mainly against the dollar, as US short rates rose; against the euro it has been supported by oil prices around 95 dollars. The 12-month differential in government rates against the United States is only 0.13 percentage points, which leaves little cushion if the dollar strengthens further.

**Differentials.** Against the euro area: 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.82 percentage points at twelve months in government rates (the stand-in for the swap differential) and 0.94 percentage points at ten years.
