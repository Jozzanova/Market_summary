# Week in review, week 46 (9–13 November 2026)

**The week was shaped by a renewed repricing of the Fed. A firmer-than-expected October CPI and a series of Fed officials arguing for further hikes pushed US short yields up by the most since 2024 and made a December hike fully priced. Equities nonetheless had their best week since August, while the leadership crisis at Nvidia turned the semiconductor sector volatile towards the end of the week.**

## Equities

- **Broad gains despite higher yields.** The S&P 500 rose 2.50 per cent, the Nasdaq Composite 2.27 per cent and the Dow 2.07 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). STOXX Europe 600 rose 2.44 per cent and the DAX 3.60 per cent.
- **Semiconductors volatile.** SOX rose 8.1 per cent over the week despite a fall of 3.4 per cent on Friday, while Nvidia lost about 13 per cent after Jensen Huang's resignation as chief executive ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Energy (+5.4 per cent) was the strongest US sector.
- **Oslo Børs** rose 2.85 per cent, helped by oil prices and Equinor ([DN](https://www.dn.no/bors/oslo-bors/hydrogenpro/bitcoin/oslo-bors-endte-opp-nar-tre-prosent-for-uken/2-1-2073730)).

## Rates

- **United States:** the 5-year yield rose 33 basis points to 4.98 per cent and the 10-year 30 basis points to 5.15 per cent, the worst week for Treasuries since April 2025 ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7bebace2-dc87-410a-ae29-ffd9f3176def)). Fed funds futures moved from partial to full pricing of a Fed hike on 9 December; CME data show the probability of 50 basis points cumulatively by January rising to 67 per cent from 16 per cent ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449075/us-treasuries-two-year-yields-headed-for-biggest-weekly-jump-in-two-years)).
- **Euro area and Germany:** the German 2-year rose 18 basis points and the 10-year 14 basis points; Bundesbank president Joachim Nagel called a further ECB hike "highly probable" ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/nagel-says-highly-probable-that-ecb-will-have-to-act-again)).
- **Norway:** the 10-year yield was unchanged over the week to Thursday at 4.31 per cent.

## Currencies

The dollar index rose 1.1 per cent, EUR/USD fell 1.6 per cent and USD/JPY rose 1.5 per cent to just below 160. EUR/NOK rose 1.2 per cent to 10.568 and USD/NOK 2.8 per cent to 9.564; I-44 rose 0.5 per cent to 109.34. The 12-month differential in government rates against the euro area was little changed at 1.82 percentage points; the krone's weakness reflected the dollar's rate advantage more than domestic factors.

## Commodities

Brent fell 2.5 per cent to 95.84 dollars as the IEA found the market in surplus ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/20b68fe2-9d60-4c0c-b714-1f3e03015823)); TTF gas rose 6.3 per cent to 90.95 euros. Gold fell 4.6 per cent and silver 5.4 per cent as real yields rose; copper fell 2.2 per cent.

## Norway

October CPI came in at 3.6 per cent, slightly below the consensus, and the economists quoted afterwards expect Norges Bank to hold in December ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)). Statistics Norway's investment survey lowered the estimate for petroleum investment in 2026 to 266 billion kroner ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/energi-og-industri/industri-og-bergverksdrift/statistikk/investeringer-i-olje-og-gass-industri-bergverk-og-kraftforsyning/artikler/moderat-nedgang-i-oljeinvesteringene-i-2026)). On Friday the Supreme Court ruled that the approvals of three oil fields must be reassessed ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/M5gz2r/klimasoeksmaalet-tillatelser-maa-behandles-paa-nytt)).

## The week ahead

Nvidia's results on Tuesday, UK CPI and the FOMC minutes on Wednesday, Norges Bank's Expectations Survey and the APEC summit with the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting on Thursday, and the deadline for a Norwegian budget agreement on Friday. Fed funds futures price about 26 basis points of Fed tightening on 9 December; the latest reported pricing for Norges Bank on 17 December is about 35 per cent for a hike.
