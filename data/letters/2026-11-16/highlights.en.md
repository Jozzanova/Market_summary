# Key points, Monday 16 November 2026

**Equities.** These reports cover Friday's session and the weekend. On Friday the S&P 500 fell 0.41 per cent and the Nasdaq 0.91 per cent, while the Dow rose 0.10 per cent; all three rose 2.1–2.5 per cent over the week ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). SOX fell 3.43 per cent, with AMD down 8.8 per cent; Nvidia lost about 13 per cent over the week ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). STOXX Europe 600 was unchanged and Oslo Børs rose 0.25 per cent, led by Equinor ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/Qroo2P/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). This morning the Nikkei is down 2.1 per cent and KOSPI 3.2 per cent as chip shares follow the US fall, while the Hang Seng is up 0.8 per cent ([AP](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/35786-stocks-decline-in-asia-as-investor-concerns-over-nvidia-fallout-extend-into-another-week/)).

**Government bonds.** On Friday the US 10-year yield rose 5 basis points to 5.15 per cent and the 5-year 4 basis points to 4.98 per cent; over the week the 5-year rose 33 basis points, the worst week for Treasuries since April 2025 ([Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40449075/us-treasuries-two-year-yields-headed-for-biggest-weekly-jump-in-two-years)). The German 10-year rose 7 basis points to 3.43 per cent. Fed funds futures price a Fed hike of about 26 basis points on 9 December and 20 more by 27 January.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 103.44, EUR/USD 1.102 and USD/JPY 159.6. EUR/NOK is 10.553, 0.14 per cent below Friday's close, and USD/NOK 9.576; I-44 was 109.34 at Friday's fix, 0.5 per cent weaker over the week, as the krone weakened against a dollar supported by rising US rates.

**Energy.** Brent is 95.27 dollars, 0.6 per cent below Friday's close: Iran's drone attack on a US base in Bahrain on Sunday has been outweighed by the IEA's estimate of a supply surplus ([Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/oil-prices-fall-on-expectations-of-coming-supply-surplus.html)). TTF gas fell 1.4 per cent on Friday to 90.95 euros.

**Drivers.** Boston Fed president Susan Collins argued for "a steady, gradual path" of hikes ([Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/feds-collins-says-gradual-rate-154619000.html)); Bank of England chief economist Huw Pill said "we have further to go" ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/14/interest-rate-rises-inflation-bank-of-england-chief-economist)); Japan's GDP fell at an annualised 0.1 per cent in the third quarter ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/japan-q3-gdp-energy-oil-prices.html)) and Chinese activity data for October missed expectations ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/china-retail-sales-october-industrial-output-investment.html)); the FT reported that Jensen Huang is discussing a return to Nvidia ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-15/huang-discusses-comeback-as-nvidia-chief-with-allies-ft-says)); Trump leaves for Asia today ahead of Thursday's meeting with Xi and Putin ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-meet-putin-xi-china-week-us-officials/story?id=138128914)).

The Supreme Court ruling on three oil fields has entered the budget talks ([NTB](https://www.adressa.no/nyheter/innenriks/i/z0GgVw/mdg-krever-full-oljestans-etter-klimadom)); external trade for October is due at 08:00.
