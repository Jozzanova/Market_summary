# Key points, Saturday 14 November 2026

**Equities.** On Friday the S&P 500 fell 0.41 per cent and the Nasdaq 0.91 per cent, while the Dow rose 0.10 per cent; all three gained 2.1–2.5 per cent over the week ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). SOX fell 3.43 per cent: AMD fell 8.75 per cent and Broadcom 4.76 per cent, and Nvidia lost about 2 per cent and 10.3 per cent on the week ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/9Bvmmr/wall-street-aapner-opp)); consumer staples rose 1.4 per cent. STOXX Europe 600 was unchanged and Oslo Børs rose 0.25 per cent, led by Equinor (+1.87 per cent) ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/Qroo2P/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). In Asia the Nikkei rose 0.53 per cent and the Hang Seng fell 1.54 per cent.

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield rose 5 basis points to 5.15 per cent and the 5-year 4 basis points to 4.98 per cent; over the week the 5-year rose 33 basis points ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7bebace2-dc87-410a-ae29-ffd9f3176def)). The German 10-year rose 7 basis points to 3.43 per cent. Markets price a Fed hike on 9 December as certain, with about 13 per cent for 50 basis points ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)).

**Currencies.** The dollar index rose 0.23 per cent to 103.41, the highest since April 2025, EUR/USD fell to 1.105 and USD/JPY is 159.9. EUR/NOK fell 0.30 per cent to 10.568 and USD/NOK rose 0.11 per cent to 9.564; I-44 was 109.34. Firm oil supported the krone against the euro.

**Energy.** Brent was unchanged at 95.84 dollars; the IEA finds that the oil market has moved into surplus as Gulf shipments recover ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/20b68fe2-9d60-4c0c-b714-1f3e03015823)). TTF gas fell 1.4 per cent to 90.95 euros.

**Drivers.** US producer prices rose 0.3 per cent in October and 6.5 per cent on the year, with core prices up 0.5 per cent ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm)); Chicago Fed president Austan Goolsbee said the Fed must do more to restore inflation credibility ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/goolsbee-says-fed-must-reestablish-its-inflation-credibility)); Bundesbank president Joachim Nagel called a further ECB hike highly probable ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/nagel-says-highly-probable-that-ecb-will-have-to-act-again)); euro-area GDP rose 0.4 per cent in the third quarter ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-13112026-ap)); a Trump–Xi–Putin meeting is set for 19 November ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/trump-to-meet-with-xi-putin-nov-19-in-china-white-house-says)).

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.82 points at twelve months in government rates (2 basis points narrower) and 0.94 points at ten years. The Supreme Court ruled 6–5 that the approvals of three oil fields, including Yggdrasil, must be reassessed; production continues ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/M5gz2r/klimasoeksmaalet-tillatelser-maa-behandles-paa-nytt)). No economist changed view on Norges Bank; the last reported pricing is about 35 per cent for a December hike.
