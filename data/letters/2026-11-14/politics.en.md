# Politics, weekly report week 46 (6–13 November 2026)

**Two political themes reached markets during the week. The US line on Iran shifted twice, first lowering and then raising the risk premium in oil, while the Strait of Hormuz remained closed. The Nvidia affair became a question of national security and export controls in Washington, and weighed on semiconductor shares in the United States and China ahead of a Trump–Xi–Putin meeting on 19 November. Diplomacy on Ukraine resumed without a breakthrough.**

## Middle East and energy

**Iran.** On Sunday and Monday President Trump said renewed strikes on Iran were "very unlikely", and Brent fell about 6 dollars on Monday ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/0B5rAG/braatt-fall-i-oljeprisen)). Tehran denied that talks were under way and repeated that the strait stays closed until its conditions are met ([Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/irans-top-security-official-says-165926144.html)). On Thursday Trump wrote that "we don't need a deal with Iran", which the FT reads as an end to Vice President Vance's push for a peace agreement ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/3c1727bb-2966-4e5c-808b-873d14854fa2)), and Brent rose 3.3 per cent. On Friday Iran launched a drone at Bahrain, which the US intercepted ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-escalates-low-level-conflict-with-a-shot-at-bahrain-73ff9b51)); oil reacted little. The market channel is the risk premium in oil: physical supply was unchanged through the week, so the price moved with the perceived probability of escalation, while the IEA's finding of a surplus as Gulf shipments recover limits the upside ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/20b68fe2-9d60-4c0c-b714-1f3e03015823)). For Norway, the oil price works through the terms of trade and the krone.

## United States

**Nvidia and export controls.** Trump said on Monday that the FBI is "looking into" Jensen Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese spy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/cbabf2da-2079-4b69-88e3-0ba84cf8d6fd)). Two Democratic senators asked the administration to preserve records on chip exports to China ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/senators-ask-trump-administration-to-preserve-nvidia-documents)), lawmakers pressed Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick on sales to a company linked to the case ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/190e5eea-7461-4b26-8b69-17a3288e31d4)), and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said his department had long favoured blacklisting Megaspeed International ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/bessent-says-treasury-favored-megaspeed-on-trade-blacklist)). China warned against using the scandal as a pretext for new restrictions ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/66e7337e-3696-4259-84f4-57533bc185bd)). The channel is the probability of tighter controls on AI chips, which weighed on semiconductor shares beyond Nvidia on Friday.

**Congress and fiscal policy.** The House voted 211–204 to block an impeachment resolution against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth ([TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/11/10/hegseth-impeachment-procedural-vote-house/)), and Democrats won a Senate seat in Mississippi ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/democrat-colom-wins-mississippi-senate-race-cnn-projects)). Federal funding expires on 11 December, two days after the Fed meeting. Trump threatened to double tariffs on Mexico ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/b4bb6e99-97c8-4392-a227-b7b74853ba26)), and a White House official said on tariffs that "we are just getting started" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/trump-tariffs-midterms-gary-cohn-inflation.html)), which keeps goods prices up while the Fed tightens.

## Asia

The White House confirmed on Friday that Trump will meet Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on 19 November on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/trump-to-meet-with-xi-putin-nov-19-in-china-white-house-says)). Bessent described the aim as de-risking, not decoupling ([AP](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/treasury-secretary/3FCJH7SWIAZG3CV42VJI55YFNM/)). The US–China trade truce expires in January; an extension would lower the risk of new tariffs and of disruption to the semiconductor supply chain. China's trade surplus passed one trillion dollars for January–October ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/a267d321-a489-4426-940e-1e4be712dc09)).

## Europe

Russia and Ukraine held their first US-mediated talks in nine months in Abu Dhabi, which ended with a prisoner exchange but no breakthrough ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/39254048-0d84-47b8-bb97-4b9d9e1f2b92)); US envoys then met President Putin ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/zelenskyy-hails-progress-after-witkoff-kushner-meet-putin)). A credible path to a ceasefire would lower the risk premium in European gas. The Bundestag approved a 2027 budget with higher borrowing ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/german-lawmakers-sign-off-on-higher-borrowing-spending-for-2027)), adding to the supply of Bunds, and Germany will propose candidates for the top ECB posts next week ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/germany-to-make-proposal-for-top-ecb-jobs-next-week-merz-says)).

## Norway

The Supreme Court ruled 6–5 on Friday that the approvals of Tyrving, Breidablikk and Yggdrasil are invalid because emissions were not assessed together; the state must reassess them, but production continues ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/M5gz2r/klimasoeksmaalet-tillatelser-maa-behandles-paa-nytt)). The ruling is likely to lengthen future approval processes. No settlement in the 2027 budget talks was reported; fuel taxes and Norgespris are the main points of disagreement ([NTB](https://www.adressa.no/nyheter/innenriks/i/64r2we/fakta-om-budsjettsamtalene)).
