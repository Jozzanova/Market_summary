# Politics, Saturday 14 November 2026

**The political news since Friday morning bears on markets mainly through US–China relations and export policy for AI chips, with a three-way meeting between Presidents Trump, Xi and Putin now confirmed for 19 November. In Norway, the Supreme Court's ruling on three oil fields does not stop production but adds a procedural requirement to future approvals.**

## United States

**Summit diplomacy.** The White House said President Trump will meet Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on 19 November on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/trump-to-meet-with-xi-putin-nov-19-in-china-white-house-says)). Officials told the FT that the President will not raise the espionage questions surrounding Nvidia with Xi ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/6034dd16-8c7d-4b3e-bc4e-942a6c8529dd)). The market channels are the US–China trade truce, which expires in January, and the prospects for a settlement in Ukraine, which bear on the risk premium in European gas.

**Export controls.** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said his department had long supported putting Megaspeed International on the trade blacklist, but that the responsibility lay with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/bessent-says-treasury-favored-megaspeed-on-trade-blacklist)). The dispute inside the administration raises the probability of tighter chip controls, which weighed on semiconductor shares in the US and on Chinese AI shares ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/0d4e93e4-895e-4ae0-a01b-833a3ed440a8)).

**Administration and infrastructure.** National Economic Council director Kevin Hassett denied a report that he plans to leave his post ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/kevin-hassett-says-he-will-remain-as-trump-economic-adviser)). On Friday evening the aviation authority grounded all flights nationwide after a communications failure in air traffic control; the order was lifted overnight ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/c9b119ff-d1da-46a7-b2b3-4ad0544fff6b)). The effect on airline shares will be visible on Monday.

## Europe

**Ukraine.** Moscow said Europe must choose an envoy if it wants a seat at the negotiating table; the EU's foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said President Putin does not want to talk to her ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/4c1f5051-94eb-4d20-b154-c45f4538a2a2)).

**Germany and Italy.** The Bundestag approved a 2027 budget with higher borrowing ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/german-lawmakers-sign-off-on-higher-borrowing-spending-for-2027)), and Germany will propose candidates for the top ECB posts next week ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/germany-to-make-proposal-for-top-ecb-jobs-next-week-merz-says)). S&P Global kept Italy's positive outlook, leaving open an upgrade ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/meloni-keeps-hope-of-italy-upgrade-at-s-p-to-highest-since-2011)), which would support a narrower periphery spread.

## Middle East and energy

Iran launched a drone at Bahrain, the first known attack on the country in two months; the US said it intercepted it ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-escalates-low-level-conflict-with-a-shot-at-bahrain-73ff9b51)). The oil price reacted little, which suggests that the market weighs the IEA's finding of a surplus as Gulf shipments recover ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/20b68fe2-9d60-4c0c-b714-1f3e03015823)) more heavily than isolated incidents.

## Norway

**The climate case.** The Supreme Court ruled 6–5 that the approvals of Tyrving, Breidablikk and Yggdrasil are invalid because emissions were not assessed together with those of the rest of the shelf ([E24](https://e24.no/energi-og-klima/i/M5gz2r/klimasoeksmaalet-tillatelser-maa-behandles-paa-nytt), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/8535fe3e-f48b-43ac-b14c-946cd5aa1d51)). The state must reassess the approvals, but operations continue; Aker BP keeps the target of first oil from Yggdrasil in the summer of 2027, and energy minister Terje Aasland said petroleum policy is unchanged. The ruling is likely to lengthen future approval processes, a modest downside risk to petroleum investment beyond 2027, while Aker BP's shares rose on Friday.

**Budget.** Labour's deadline for a 2027 budget agreement with the other parties is Friday 20 November; no report of a settlement could be found.
