# Markets, Saturday 14 November 2026

**Friday's session was shaped by two forces pulling in different directions: a renewed fall in semiconductor shares after the leadership change at Nvidia, and a further rise in Treasury yields as the October producer prices confirmed that inflation pressure remains broad. Investors moved out of chipmakers and into defensive sectors, so the broad indices fell only modestly and still closed their best week since early August. Today is Saturday; this report covers Friday's session and the news since.**

## Equities

**United States.** The S&P 500 fell 0.41 per cent and the Nasdaq Composite 0.91 per cent, while the Dow rose 0.10 per cent and the Russell 2000 fell 0.31 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Over the week the S&P 500 rose 2.5 per cent, the Nasdaq 2.3 per cent and the Dow 2.1 per cent (same source). Nvidia fell about 2 per cent and lost 10.3 per cent over the week, roughly 450 billion dollars of market value ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/9Bvmmr/wall-street-aapner-opp)); HSBC downgraded the stock to hold ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)), and the FT reports that Arm's chief executive Rene Haas has been approached in the search for a successor ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/2a64f698-46dd-4178-a3f4-4c5f4c0a0926)).

**The AI and semiconductor proxies fell markedly more than the market.** SOX fell 3.43 per cent and the SOXX ETF 3.67 per cent, as the shares that had gained on Nvidia's troubles earlier in the week reversed: AMD fell 8.75 per cent and Broadcom 4.76 per cent ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/9Bvmmr/wall-street-aapner-opp)). NYSE FANG+ fell 1.00 per cent and the AI ETF AIQ 1.29 per cent, while the Magnificent Seven ETF was little changed (−0.29 per cent), held up by Apple, which touched a record intraday ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Technology was the weakest sector (−2.0 per cent); consumer staples (+1.4 per cent), communication services (+1.1 per cent) and energy (+1.0 per cent) rose, a pattern consistent with a rotation towards less rate- and AI-sensitive sectors. Cisco fell about 6 per cent despite raising its guidance. VIX rose 0.6 points to 19.3. The WSJ notes that next week, with Nvidia's results on 17 November and the Anthropic listing, brings the main tests of the AI trade ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/tech-stocks-fall-as-next-week-brings-big-tests-for-the-ai-trade-6a346b49)).

**Europe and Norway.** STOXX Europe 600 was unchanged (+0.02 per cent), Euro Stoxx 50 fell 0.19 per cent and the FTSE 100 rose 0.36 per cent; European indices turned positive in the morning as oil fell ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/nikkei-rises-1-1-led-by-chip-stocks-eb2ed881)). Over the week STOXX 600 rose 2.4 per cent and the DAX 3.6 per cent. Oslo Børs rose 0.25 per cent, led by oil and tankers: Equinor rose 1.87 per cent and Okeanis Eco Tankers 1.99 per cent after an upgrade, while Hydrogenpro fell 24.2 per cent after saying it needs new funding ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/Qroo2P/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). Aker BP rose 0.74 per cent although the Supreme Court declared the approval of three fields invalid (see Politics).

**Asia.** On Friday the Nikkei rose 0.53 per cent, while the Hang Seng fell 1.54 per cent and the KOSPI 0.98 per cent; Chinese AI shares fell on concern about tighter US chip restrictions ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/0d4e93e4-895e-4ae0-a01b-833a3ed440a8)). There is no Asian session today.

## Fixed income and credit

The 10-year Treasury yield rose 5 basis points to 5.15 per cent, the 5-year 4 basis points to 4.98 per cent and the 30-year 5 basis points to 5.48 per cent. Over the week the 5-year rose 33 basis points and the 10-year 30 basis points; the FT describes it as the worst week for Treasuries since April 2025 ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7bebace2-dc87-410a-ae29-ffd9f3176def)). CME FedWatch shows a hike on 9 December as fully priced, with about 87 per cent for 25 basis points and 13 per cent for 50 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)); Bloomberg reports that the debate has moved on to January ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/fed-set-to-raise-interest-rates-next-month-as-debate-moves-to-january)). In Europe, German yields rose: the 2-year 4 basis points to 3.13 per cent and the 10-year 7 basis points to 3.43 per cent, after Bundesbank president Joachim Nagel said further ECB tightening is highly probable ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/nagel-says-highly-probable-that-ecb-will-have-to-act-again)).

## Commodities and currencies

**Energy.** Brent was unchanged at 95.84 dollars after falling in the European morning. The IEA's monthly report finds that the oil market has swung into surplus as Gulf shipments recover, though stocks will take months to rebuild ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/20b68fe2-9d60-4c0c-b714-1f3e03015823)). An Iranian drone attack on Bahrain, intercepted by the US, was a reminder of the risk to supply ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-escalates-low-level-conflict-with-a-shot-at-bahrain-73ff9b51)). TTF gas fell 1.4 per cent to 90.95 euros.

**Metals.** Gold was unchanged at 3,886 dollars and is 4.6 per cent lower over a week of rising yields; silver fell 1.9 per cent and copper 1.7 per cent.

**Currencies.** The dollar index rose 0.23 per cent to 103.41, its highest since April 2025 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)); EUR/USD fell 0.41 per cent to 1.105 and USD/JPY is 159.9. The krone strengthened against the euro: EUR/NOK fell 0.30 per cent to 10.568, while USD/NOK rose 0.11 per cent to 9.564. I-44 (a rise is a weaker krone) was 109.34 at Friday's fix, 0.06 per cent higher. Firm oil prices are the likely support against the euro, while the wider US rate advantage favoured the dollar. Bitcoin fell 6.2 per cent and traded below 70,000 dollars for the first time since August ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)).
