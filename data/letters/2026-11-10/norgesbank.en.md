# Norges Bank, Tuesday 10 November 2026

**Summary.** Inflation in October was 3.6 per cent, a notch below the 3.7 consensus, and CPI-ATE fell to 2.7 per cent from 3.0, as expected and in line with Norges Bank's projection. Economists read the print as confirmation that inflation is following the path the bank has assumed: Handelsbanken, SpareBank 1 Markets and SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge all expect an unchanged rate in December, and several judge that the peak has been reached. The krone nevertheless weakened through the day, to 10.62 against the euro, as Brent fell below 90 dollars.

## New data

- CPI rose 3.6 per cent on twelve months in October (3.4 in September) and 0.3 per cent on the month. CPI-ATE rose 2.7 per cent (3.0), also 0.3 per cent on the month. Consensus was 3.7 and 2.7 ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/priser-og-prisindekser/konsumpriser/statistikk/konsumprisindeksen)).
- Electricity explains the rise in headline inflation: electricity including grid fees fell 0.4 per cent from September, against 8.3 per cent in October last year, when the Norgespris scheme and a lower electricity tax took effect. This is a base effect, not new price pressure. Food and non-alcoholic drinks fell 0.4 per cent on the month, the third fall in a row, and are 1.4 per cent higher than a year ago; imported goods are 0.7 per cent higher. Goods rose 2.8 per cent and services 4.1 per cent ([SSB](https://www.ssb.no/priser-og-prisindekser/konsumpriser/statistikk/konsumprisindeksen), [E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)).
- The fall in core inflation is partly a base effect as well, since the strong monthly rise of October 2025 dropped out of the twelve-month rate ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)). The underlying picture is nonetheless that goods inflation is easing, while services prices are still rising faster than is consistent with the target over time.

## What others say

- Marius Gonsholt Hov, Handelsbanken: the print "does not change rate expectations now". It "clearly looks like" an unchanged rate for the rest of the year; a hike before Christmas would have required "a fairly strong upside" in today's figure and in the November one ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)).
- Harald Magnus Andreassen, SpareBank 1 Markets: lower than "we and the market expected", but as Norges Bank expected. Inflation is now on the path the bank assumed, and he sees neither a hike in December nor next year ([Frifagbevegelse/NTB](https://www.frifagbevegelse.no/nyheter/okonomer-avlyser-renteokning-i-ar/2732723)).
- Kyrre M. Knudsen, SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge: "This is good news". He expects an unchanged rate in December and a signal that the peak has been reached; a stronger krone and oil below 90 dollars pull inflation further down ([TV 2](https://www.tv2.no/nyheter/mener-prisveksten-kan-vaere-gode-nyheter/19412716/), [Nettavisen](https://www.nettavisen.no/okonomi/alt-blir-dyrere-kpi-pa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober/s/80-95-235224)).
- E24 sums up that several economists lean towards the peak having been reached, citing the stronger krone and lower rate expectations abroad ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/M5gG7R/prisveksten-laa-paa-3-6-prosent-i-oktober)). Before the print the market priced about a 35 per cent probability of a December hike ([Estate Nyheter](https://www.estatenyheter.no/inflasjon-norges-bank-obligasjonsrenter/norges-bank-forberedt-pa-a-sette-renten-opp/522335), 5 November); no updated market pricing has been published since. None of the economists quoted expects a hike in December. Taken together, the information in today's print is that the upside risk to the policy rate has diminished, not that cuts have moved closer.

## The krone and rates

EUR/NOK is 10.62 this afternoon, 0.8 per cent higher than on Monday and 2.0 per cent higher over the week; USD/NOK is 9.49. The krone weakened after the print and further through the day as Brent fell to 89 dollars, 3.4 per cent below Monday. The move follows oil rather than rates: the 3-year government yield was 4.59 per cent and the 10-year 4.28 per cent on Monday, little changed, and the differential against the euro area is unchanged at 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate and about 1.0 point at ten years against the AAA curve. The week's weakening should be seen against an I-44 index of 108.81 at Monday's fix, 1.5 per cent weaker over the week but 9.2 per cent stronger this year. A weaker krone lifts imported inflation in isolation, but the level remains so much stronger than at the start of the year that the krone's contribution to inflation ahead is likely to stay negative. A core print on the path gives the market no reason to price a firmer rate path.
