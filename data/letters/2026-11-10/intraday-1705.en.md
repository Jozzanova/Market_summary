# Intraday update, Tuesday 10 November 2026, as of 17:05 Oslo time

**Summary.** A hotter US core inflation print has reversed the morning's calm: Treasury yields jumped, the dollar rose and US equities are lower, while European shares held modest gains and the krone weakened. US core CPI rose 0.3 per cent in October against 0.2 expected, and the market now prices a December Fed hike with Fed speakers debating its size. The S&P 500 is 0.7 per cent lower, the US 10-year yield about 13 basis points higher at 4.99 per cent, and EUR/NOK 0.8 per cent higher at 10.63.
