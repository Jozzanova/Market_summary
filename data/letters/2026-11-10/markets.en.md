# Markets, Tuesday 10 November 2026

**Summary.** Equities rebounded broadly on Monday as semiconductor shares recovered from last week's sell-off and a sharp fall in oil eased inflation concerns: the S&P 500 rose 2.35 per cent to 7,550, the Nasdaq Composite 2.74 per cent and the semiconductor index (SOX) 6.9 per cent, while Nvidia fell about 4 per cent on its board's investigation of its chief executive. The rally carried into Asia this morning, with the KOSPI up 3.8 per cent and the Nikkei 225 2.6 per cent. Brent has fallen a further 1.2 per cent to 91.4 dollars on hopes of de-escalation with Iran, although Tehran has dismissed reports of talks. Consumer prices in Norway (08:00) and the United States (14:30) are today's tests.

## Equities

- **United States.** All eleven S&P 500 sectors rose, led by industrials (+3.8 per cent), technology (+3.7 per cent) and consumer discretionary (+2.9 per cent); energy (+0.5 per cent) and staples (+0.9 per cent) lagged as investors rotated back towards risk. AMD rose about 13 per cent and Broadcom nearly 9 per cent, while Nvidia was the only decliner in the semiconductor index after its board opened an investigation into whether a foreign intelligence effort targeted Jensen Huang ([Reuters via Kitco](http://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-11-09/wall-st-jumps-back-oil-slide-eases-inflation-worries-nvidia-slumps), [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-9-2026)). President Trump said the FBI is "looking into" the matter ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/cbabf2da-2079-4b69-88e3-0ba84cf8d6fd)). The AI proxies outperformed the market but remain well below their October levels: the SOX is still 20.2 per cent lower over the month, NYSE FANG+ rose 3.1 per cent, the Magnificent Seven ETF 2.3 per cent and the AI ETFs between 2.7 and 4.0 per cent. Morgan Stanley and UBS called last week's sell-off overdone. The VIX fell 22 per cent to 19.9.
- **Europe.** The STOXX Europe 600 rose 1.3 per cent, the Euro Stoxx 50 1.9 per cent and the DAX 1.7 per cent.
- **Norway.** The benchmark index rose 1.27 per cent to 2,079.5, led by Kongsberg Gruppen (+4.2 per cent); Autostore rose about 11 per cent after an upgrade from DNB Carnegie, while Equinor fell 2.6 per cent and Aker BP 1.2 per cent with the oil price ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/ez1gpO/oppgang-paa-oslo-boers)). The index is 23.2 per cent higher this year.
- **Asia.** Chipmakers led again: Samsung gained 4.7 per cent and SK Hynix 6.7 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/ai-softbank-sk-hynix-samsung-wall-street-tech-stocks.html)), taking the KOSPI up 4.4 per cent to 5,239, still about a fifth below its level a month ago. The Hang Seng rose 0.6 per cent and Shanghai 0.2 per cent ([AP via Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/11/09/stock-markets-ai-earnings-oil/8cb975d8-dcb9-11f1-912c-4fbb80d73f7f_story.html)).

## Fixed income and credit

- Treasuries were little changed despite the equity rally: the 10-year yield is 4.86 per cent and the 5-year 4.66 per cent on live prints, as investors await the inflation report ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/inflation-data-to-test-limits-of-treasury-rally-after-haven-bid)). At the latest official fixes the 2s10s curve stood at 35 basis points, the euro-area AAA 10-year yield at 3.29 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year at 4.29 per cent.
- US high-yield spreads narrowed 6 basis points to 342 basis points. Japan's 30-year auction drew weaker demand than average ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/japan-30-year-bond-sale-sees-weaker-demand-than-12-month-average-mwa53xcl)).

## Commodities and currencies

- **Oil.** Brent fell about 6 dollars during Monday after Trump said new strikes on Iran were "very unlikely" ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/0B5rAG/braatt-fall-i-oljeprisen)), and is at 91.4 dollars this morning, 12.7 per cent lower over the week. Iran's Mohsen Rezaei said no negotiations are taking place and that the Strait of Hormuz will not reopen until the US meets Iran's seven conditions ([Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/irans-top-security-official-says-165926144.html)); Rystad's Claudio Galimberti noted that Gulf exports remain 20 per cent below pre-war levels. European gas (TTF) fell 3.6 per cent to 82.5 euros.
- **Metals.** Gold is at 4,124 dollars, little changed; copper rose 0.4 per cent, tracking the technology rebound ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/copper-extends-rebound-as-global-technology-stocks-resume-rally)).
- **FX.** The dollar index is at 101.7 and EUR/USD at 1.123; USD/JPY is near 158.
- **The krone.** EUR/NOK is at 10.55, unchanged from Monday evening, and USD/NOK at 9.39, 0.7 per cent lower as the dollar softened. The krone has not benefited from the equity rally, plausibly because of the lower oil price, though no source attributes the move: EUR/NOK is 1.3 per cent higher over the week, and the I-44 index stood at 108.81 on Monday, 1.5 per cent weaker over the week but 9.2 per cent stronger this year.
