# Politics, Tuesday 10 November 2026

**Summary.** The Iran conflict remained the main political driver of markets. President Trump said after a call with Qatar's prime minister that renewed military action looks unlikely, which pushed oil lower, but Tehran dismissed reports of talks and repeated that the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until its conditions are met. In Washington, the Nvidia affair has become a political issue, and Democrats are weighing whether to use the 11 December funding deadline to constrain the war. China's trade surplus passed one trillion dollars ahead of an expected Trump–Xi meeting next week.

## Middle East and energy

Trump said he sees "positive momentum" towards ending the conflict and that renewed US military action is unlikely, while noting that Gulf states would prefer to keep the US naval blockade in place. Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, called the talks "fake news" and demanded that Washington accept seven conditions, including an end to the war on all fronts, the release of frozen funds and the lifting of the blockade ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/commodities/iran-tells-us-to-stop-fake-news-on-talks-demands-it-accept-seven-conditions-to-end-war/)). Secretary of State Rubio called Tehran's demands unacceptable, and attacks continued, including a fire on a Saudi east-west pipeline on Monday ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/0B5rAG/braatt-fall-i-oljeprisen)). US refuelling aircraft were moved from a second UK base because of an Iranian threat ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/u-s-aircraft-evacuated-from-second-u-k-base-due-to-iran-threat-835a8b58)). The market channel is the oil price: Brent has fallen to 91.4 dollars, but the gap between the two sides' positions limits the scope for a lasting decline.

## United States

- **Nvidia.** Trump said the FBI is "looking into" chief executive Jensen Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese spy ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/cbabf2da-2079-4b69-88e3-0ba84cf8d6fd)), and members of the House China committee have asked for answers. The channel is export controls on chips to China, which Huang has lobbied to loosen ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/how-jensen-huangs-affair-spiraled-into-a-national-security-nightmare-d9b43304)).
- **Congress.** Democrats are discussing restrictions on Iran war funding in the bill needed by 11 December to avoid a shutdown ([Asia Times](https://asiatimes.com/2026/11/as-trump-renews-iran-nuke-threats-dems-ponder-blocking-war-funds/)). Republican representative Thomas Massie introduced articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Hegseth, whom Trump defended ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-10/hegseth-hits-out-at-critics-as-massie-pushes-impeachment-video)).
