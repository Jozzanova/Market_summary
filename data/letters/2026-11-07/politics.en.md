# Politics, Saturday 7 November 2026

**Summary.** The political consequences of Tuesday's US midterm elections continued to shape markets. President Trump replaced his chief of staff, Susie Wiles, with Stephen Miller, while his advisers remain divided over whether to end or resume the war with Iran. Washington also pressed Beijing to honour the Busan trade agreement, and China vowed to retaliate against new US sanctions. In Europe the race to lead the ECB has narrowed to two candidates. In Norway, the Finance Minister called the fall in the oil fund's value manageable.

## United States

- **Midterms and the AI trade.** Democrats' victories, described by Bloomberg as a "disastrous" defeat for the Republican Party, intensified concern about the regulatory outlook for AI investment; the WSJ reports that systematic trading amplified the resulting losses ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/trump-touts-plan-for-us-air-show-seeks-to-turn-page-on-midterm), [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/midterms-markets-week-ai-trade-97b3fc5d), [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2026/11/05/markets-realise-they-hate-americas-blue-wave)).
- **White House.** Trump named Stephen Miller chief of staff; Wiles moves to his political operation ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/6e8f2244-0e39-45cd-b415-e0be54b0351f)). The market channel is policy continuity on immigration and trade, which bears on labour supply and inflation.
- **Nvidia.** US intelligence officials told the administration that a woman close to Nvidia's chief executive was working for China, according to the New York Times ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/us-believes-woman-close-to-nvidia-ceo-was-a-china-spy-nyt-says)). The report adds to the headline risk around the largest AI stock; there was no market reaction to assess at the time of writing.

## Middle East and energy

Vice President Vance and Gulf allies are advising the President to end the war, while other advisers argue for renewed strikes ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/trumps-clashing-advisers-press-him-to-make-iran-war-decision-758c1e55)). Iran has sent a proposal via Qatar that would allow free passage through the Strait of Hormuz, conditional on demands including an end to the naval blockade; Washington is reviewing it ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-oil-midterms.html)). The US also sanctioned eight Canadian entities over Iran-related transactions ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/us-sanctions-eight-canadian-companies-over-iran-transactions)). The channel is oil: Brent fell 16 per cent this week to 98 dollars on de-escalation hopes.

## Asia

Washington warned China that it must "immediately" meet the terms of the Busan agreement on rare earths and other critical products ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/391bb785-0247-4c39-b53b-35007187d603)), and Beijing said it reserves the right to respond to US sanctions on two Chinese companies ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/beijing-vows-to-hit-back-against-us-sanctions-on-china-companies)). New Zealand votes today in an election dominated by the cost of living ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/1e80651b-14f3-4cf0-9b47-2f6dee158496)).

## Europe

Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernández de Cos are the only candidates to succeed Christine Lagarde; leaders aim to decide in December ([Global Banking & Finance/Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/central-banking-veterans-knot-de-cos-race-replace-lagarde/)). The EU has rejected a US push for a new security arrangement in Europe ([DN](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiogFBVV95cUxQSzhSVlFPWU9ZamhobEtPSmNiam1pU3NPbk9OY1ZTcHl5RC1KbGVJUEpvblJRZjBmdlphNDNBR1dYS2RaWktOaGtNRXFhYlZUckk4S3pQYU9LTFc3bVdSSkt2aEZnRmlGVy1tUzdUal9OdDhfUEFIVS1sOU8ycDRkelJ4TEo0TFM2M29wdUowZE5KTUhBYS14cXN6S29Sa1ZtSUE?oc=5)).

## Norway

Finance Minister Jens Stoltenberg said that "it is never good that the fund falls in value, but it is manageable", and that diversification across many markets is the best way to manage risk ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/779OWv/finansministeren-om-boersuro-det-er-aldri-bra-at-fondet-faller-i-verdi)). The fund's large technology holdings were hit hard this week; no figure for the fall in value has been published.
