# Macro, Saturday 7 November 2026

**Summary.** A strong US employment report on Friday reinforced the case for a Federal Reserve rate increase in December. Payrolls rose 165,000 in October, against about 70,000 expected, September was revised up to 60,000 and unemployment fell to 4.1 per cent. Futures price a quarter-point hike at the 8–9 December meeting with a probability of about 78 per cent. The week closed with three of the four central banks that met this week raising rates; Norges Bank held. In the euro area, the field to succeed ECB President Lagarde has narrowed to two candidates.

## Federal Reserve

- **The data.** The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported 165,000 new non-farm jobs in October, led by about 70,000 in professional and business services. Unemployment fell from 4.2 to 4.1 per cent and annual wage growth rose to 3.1 per cent from 3.0 per cent. PCE inflation was 3.6 per cent in September ([E24](https://e24.no/internasjonal-oekonomi/i/GrvbEQ/det-ble-skapt-165-000-nye-jobber-utenfor-landbruket-i-usa-i-oktober), [WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/october-jobs-report-unemployment-d7392948)).
- **The reaction.** The probability of a December hike rose to about 85 per cent immediately after the release and settled near 78 per cent, against 67 per cent on Thursday; futures now see the last hike in April rather than June. St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem has argued for further increases, saying policy must be made "for the economy of the day". Chair Kevin Warsh said last month the Committee would watch "the accumulation of data" ([Business Recorder/Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447788/fed-seen-sticking-to-december-rate-hike-after-jobs-data)). Handelsbanken's Marius Gonsholt Hov expects a December hike ([E24](https://e24.no/internasjonal-oekonomi/i/GrvbEQ/det-ble-skapt-165-000-nye-jobber-utenfor-landbruket-i-usa-i-oktober)).
- The US 10-year yield rose 2 basis points to 4.86 per cent on the day. The October consumer price report, due on 10 November according to third-party calendars, is the last before the meeting.

## ECB

Eurogroup President Kyriakos Pierrakakis said on Friday that Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernández de Cos are the only nominees to succeed Christine Lagarde, who leaves by 7 February 2027. Finance ministers discuss the candidates on 9 November, and leaders aim to agree at their 17–18 December summit, probably as a package that also covers the successors to Isabel Schnabel and Philip Lane. Analysts quoted describe Knot as slightly more hawkish and Hernández de Cos as slightly more dovish ([Global Banking & Finance/Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/central-banking-veterans-knot-de-cos-race-replace-lagarde/)). The deposit rate is 2.50 per cent; the euro-area AAA 10-year yield was 3.29 per cent at Thursday's fix.

## Assessment

The balance of central-bank risk has shifted towards further tightening outside Norway. US labour-market strength and inflation of 3.6 per cent leave the Fed inclined to raise rates, even as the equity sell-off has tightened financial conditions: high-yield spreads have widened 22 basis points in a week and the VIX is above 25. Energy remains the main two-sided risk. Brent has fallen 16 per cent in a week on hopes of US–Iran de-escalation, but remains 62 per cent higher than at the start of the year, and analysts warn that a resumption of fighting would push it back above 100 dollars ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-oil-midterms.html)). For Norway, a weaker krone works in the opposite direction to the stronger currency that underpinned Thursday's hold.
