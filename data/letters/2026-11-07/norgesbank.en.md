# Norges Bank, Saturday 7 November 2026

**Summary.** Norges Bank has published nothing further on monetary policy since Thursday's decision to keep the policy rate at 4.50 per cent. The news since then has come from the krone, which weakened again on Friday as the global equity sell-off spread to Europe: EUR/NOK ended the week at 10.54, and the import-weighted index is 2 per cent weaker than a week ago. Economists continue to see the krone as the main channel through which a December hike could return to the table, though Nordea warns that a weaker currency would not automatically mean higher rates.

## What Norges Bank said

The Bank's news page lists no press releases or speeches after 5 November ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/news/)). The standing message is Thursday's: "inflation is too high", it "will likely be necessary to keep the policy rate elevated for a time", and the Committee is "prepared to raise the policy rate if needed". The krone "has appreciated and is stronger than assumed" ([Norges Bank](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/monetary-policy/Monetary-policy-meetings/2026/november-2026/)).

## What economists and the market say

- **Nordea.** Chief economist Kjetil Olsen says the krone is still about 1 per cent stronger than Norges Bank assumed. He attributes Friday's weakening largely to life and pension companies selling kroner to rebalance their currency hedges when foreign equities fall, calling it "almost mechanical", and notes that a weaker krone would not automatically lead to higher rates, since the Bank weighs several factors that often pull in different directions ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/Pg6kg0/kronen-svekkes-etter-global-boerssmell)).
- **Thursday's reading stands.** DNB Carnegie and Handelsbanken think the peak has probably been reached, Danske Bank calls it "a knife-edge" and JP Morgan has forecast one more hike in the first quarter, as summarised in yesterday's letter ([E24](https://e24.no/norsk-oekonomi/i/p1o20V/sjefoekonom-de-holder-muligheten-aapne-for-en-heving-til), [Børsen](https://borsen.dagbladet.no/nyheter/forberedt-pa-a-sette-opp-renta/85382226)).
- **Government.** Finance Minister Jens Stoltenberg called the fall in the oil fund's value "manageable" ([E24](https://e24.no/boers-og-finans/i/779OWv/finansministeren-om-boersuro-det-er-aldri-bra-at-fondet-faller-i-verdi)).
- No new comments on Norges Bank from international banks or wire services could be found for Friday.

## The krone and rates

EUR/NOK rose 0.9 per cent on Friday to 10.54 and USD/NOK 1.7 per cent to 9.45. The I-44 index was 108.79 at Friday's fix, 1.0 per cent weaker on the day and 2.0 per cent over the week, though still 9 per cent stronger than at the start of the year. At Thursday's fixes the 3-year government bond yielded 4.60 per cent and the 10-year 4.31 per cent, 17 and 13 basis points lower over the week. Higher US rate expectations after Friday's employment report and a weaker krone both lean towards a firmer December decision; lower oil prices lean the other way.
