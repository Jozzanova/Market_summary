# Markets, Wednesday 11 November 2026

**US inflation above expectations set the tone. Treasury yields rose about 12 basis points at ten years and more at the short end, US equities fell and the dollar firmed against the euro, sterling and the yen. Europe closed before the full repricing and ended slightly higher. Brent has recovered to 92 dollars from a two-month low, and the krone weakened, most against the dollar.**

## Equities

The S&P 500 fell 0.38 per cent to 7,521 on Tuesday, the Nasdaq 0.74 per cent to 25,066 and the Dow 0.30 per cent ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). The fall was modest given the rise in yields. That suggests a hike in December was already largely expected, and that the print mainly shifted the debate towards its size. Nvidia fell 5.5 per cent and was the largest drag on the index; its board is investigating chief executive Jensen Huang ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). The semiconductor index SOX fell only 0.35 per cent, as Broadcom rose 3.9 per cent. NYSE FANG+ fell 0.49 per cent and the Magnificent Seven ETF 0.78 per cent, both a little more than the broad market. The technology sector lost 1.15 per cent, while financials and health care rose. Over a month SOX is still 20.5 per cent lower, which shows how far the AI segment has been repriced. Anthropic set a price range of 70–78 dollars a share for its listing, valuing it at up to 1.405 trillion dollars fully diluted; according to sources, pricing is on 18 November ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html)).

STOXX Europe 600 rose 0.27 per cent to 658.3, within 0.8 per cent of its record. Banks rose 1.7 per cent and oil companies fell 1.6 per cent as Brent touched a two-month low ([Reuters](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/european-shares-near-record-high-as-lower-oil-prices-boost-sentiment-ce785edadb8aff23)). Oslo Børs rose 0.09 per cent to 2,081. Lower oil weighed on energy shares, but no full report of the session's drivers was available.

In Asia, the Nikkei is up 1.3 per cent and the KOSPI 2.5 per cent this morning, led by the chip makers: AP reports Samsung up 2.3 per cent and SK Hynix up 3.4 per cent. The Hang Seng is down 0.8 per cent ([AP](https://www.boston25news.com/news/business/asian-shares-mostly/RXDWAASOVU4OJEDRZPQEZU3OZA/)). SoftBank fell after a quarterly loss of about 7 billion dollars.

## Fixed income and credit

The US 2-year yield rose 18 basis points to about 4.75 per cent, the largest daily rise since April 2025. The 10-year rose about 12 basis points to just under 5.0 per cent ([Reuters](https://investing.com/news/economy-news/bonds-reel-as-hot-inflation-fuels-jumbo-fed-hike-fears-5017511)). Most of the move came at the short end, consistent with the market bringing forward expected Fed hikes. The Japanese 10-year yield rose 7 basis points to 2.755 per cent. The latest euro-area and Norwegian official fixes are from Monday: the AAA 10-year at 3.27 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year at 4.28 per cent. Credit spreads narrowed somewhat on Monday, with the US high-yield spread at 3.34 per cent. The US bond market is closed today for Veterans Day, while equities trade as usual ([SIFMA](https://www.sifma.org/news/press-releases/sifma-fixed-income-market-close-recommendation-in-the-u-s-the-u-k-and-japan-for-the-u-s-veterans-day-holiday-2026)).

## Commodities and currencies

**Oil.** Brent fell to 91.49 dollars on Tuesday, the lowest since 31 August, after signals that the United States sees no need for further strikes on Iran ([Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/2026/11/11/oil-hits-two-month-lows-as-us-eases-iran-fears)). This morning it is 92.4 dollars, up 1.0 per cent, as new attacks on shipping near Hormuz offset hopes of a truce ([Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-rises-from-twomonth-low-as-hormuz-shipping-attacks-offset-iran-truce-hopes-5017512)). Brent is still 10.8 per cent lower on the week.

**Metals.** Gold fell almost 3 per cent on Tuesday as yields and the dollar rose, and has recovered to 4,055 dollars ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/gold-edges-higher-after-sharp-selloff-fed-hike-bets-weigh.html)).

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 102.25. EUR/USD fell 0.5 per cent to 1.117 and USD/JPY rose to 158.7, which reflects the wider differential to US rates.

**The krone.** EUR/NOK rose 0.5 per cent to 10.605 and USD/NOK 1.0 per cent to 9.497. The I-44 index rose 0.7 per cent to 109.57 at Tuesday's fix, where a rise means a weaker krone. Two factors stand out: the stronger dollar after the US print, and the weakest oil price in two months.
