# Key points, Wednesday 11 November 2026

**Equities.** US equities fell on Tuesday: the S&P 500 lost 0.38 per cent to 7,521 and the Nasdaq 0.74 per cent. STOXX Europe 600 rose 0.27 per cent and Oslo Børs 0.09 per cent. In Asia this morning the Nikkei is up 1.3 per cent and the KOSPI 2.5 per cent, while the Hang Seng is down 0.8 per cent. Nvidia fell 5.5 per cent amid the inquiry into its chief executive and pulled the Nasdaq down more than the broad market.

**Government bonds.** The US 10-year yield rose about 12 basis points to 4.98 per cent after the inflation report. The latest official fixes for Europe are from Monday: the euro-area AAA 10-year at 3.27 per cent and the Norwegian 10-year at 4.28 per cent, so neither reflects Tuesday yet. Fed funds futures now fully price a 25 basis point hike on 9 December.

**Currencies.** The dollar index is 102.25, EUR/USD 1.117 (−0.5 per cent) and USD/JPY 158.7. The krone weakened: EUR/NOK is 10.605 (+0.5 per cent), USD/NOK 9.497 (+1.0 per cent), and I-44 rose 0.7 per cent to 109.57 at Tuesday's fix, 2.7 per cent weaker on the week. The larger move against the dollar suggests that the stronger dollar after the US print, together with Tuesday's low in oil, weighed on the krone; no source attributes the move.

**Energy.** Brent is 92.4 dollars, 1.0 per cent higher after a two-month low on Tuesday. The price is held down by a lower perceived probability of escalation in the Iran conflict, while the Strait of Hormuz remains closed and physical supply is unchanged.

**Drivers.** US consumer prices rose 0.4 per cent in October and core prices 0.3 per cent, against expectations of 0.3 and 0.2 ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/cpi-inflation-report-october-2026.html)). Fed officials responded firmly: Goolsbee called the report "borderline disturbing" ([PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/u-s-inflation-report-highlights-strain-of-rising-food-energy-and-housing-costs)), and Waller said a 50 basis point hike "should be considered" in December ([Reuters](https://investing.com/news/economy-news/investors-boost-odds-of-halfpoint-fed-hike-as-core-cpi-rise-meets-wallers-bar-5016055)). At the same London conference the Bank of England's Lombardelli and the ECB's Lane pointed to further tightening ([Bloomberg](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/nov/10/officials-from-major-central-banks-signal-more-rat/)). Higher US yields and a stronger dollar therefore account for the fall in equities, the rise in yields and the weaker krone alike.

**Norway.** The differential against the euro area is unchanged at 2.00 percentage points on the policy rate, 1.93 points at twelve months in government rates and about 1.0 point at ten years. Economists quoted after Tuesday's CPI expect an unchanged rate in December; no post-print market pricing has been reported. Norges Bank publishes its Financial Stability Report this morning.
