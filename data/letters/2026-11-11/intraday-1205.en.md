# Intraday update, Wednesday 11 November 2026, as of 12:05 Oslo time

**Summary.** European equities are higher for a third day and US futures point to a rebound in technology after Tuesday's sell-off, but the dollar and oil are firmer and the krone has weakened further. Oslo Børs is up 0.9 per cent, while EUR/NOK is 0.6 per cent higher at 10.62 and USD/NOK 1.4 per cent higher at 9.53. The US bond market is closed today, so the first test of Tuesday's rise in yields comes tomorrow.
