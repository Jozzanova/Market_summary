Market summary

A daily review of equity, bond, commodity and currency markets over the last 24 hours, a macro letter on central banks, a politics letter, a Norges Bank letter and the events ahead. Written by a scheduled agent each morning from the market data and news available at 06:00 Oslo time, with an intraday update on weekdays around 12:00 and 17:00.
